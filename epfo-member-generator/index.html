EPFO Member Registration Bulk File Generator

Prepare the EPFO new-member registration bulk file. Add employees one by one or import them from Excel, JSON or an EPFO TXT file. The tool validates every record, including Aadhaar, PAN, IFSC, dates, international worker and differently abled details, and downloads the 30-field TXT file with the #~# delimiter. It works offline, saves your work in your browser, and never uploads employee data.

Questions

What does this tool do?

It prepares the EPFO member registration bulk TXT file. You add or import employees, the tool checks every record, and you download a file with exactly 30 fields per member separated by #~#.

Does it submit the file to EPFO?

No. It is an independent data preparation utility. You upload the generated file yourself on the EPFO portal after checking it.

Where is my employee data stored?

Only in your own browser, in its local database. Nothing is uploaded to Techmin or anyone else, and the tool works offline.

Can I import from Excel?

Yes. Download the Excel template or use your own sheet and map the columns. .xlsx, .xls and .csv are supported, with a preview and an error report.

Does it remember my work?

Yes. Records are saved automatically in your browser, and the SAVE button saves again at any time. Use Backup & Restore to keep a file copy.

Who must use the UMANG app instead?

As per the EPFO notice, UAN generation through the member registration module is restricted except for international workers and citizens of Nepal and Bhutan. Other new employees should generate their UAN through face authentication in the UMANG app.

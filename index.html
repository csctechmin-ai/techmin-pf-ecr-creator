Online PF ECR File Creator for Payroll Excel

Techmin HR Solutions’ PF ECR File Creator helps employers and payroll teams prepare an ECR text draft from a structured Excel payroll sheet. Upload the required payroll columns, review validation results, calculate Employee PF, EPS and Employer EPF, and download a contribution-summary PDF.

This browser-based utility is not an EPFO portal or official government service. Contribution eligibility, wage ceilings, age-related pension rules and the final ECR format must be checked against current EPFO instructions before submission.

3. Upload payroll Excel

Upload your payroll sheet and generate an EPFO-compliant ECR file instantly—ready for direct upload to the EPFO portal.

Use the Techmin Excel template with worksheet PF Data and only these 8 input columns: Universal Account No., Employee Name (As per EPFO/UIDAI), Gross Wages, PF Wages, Pension Wages, EDLI Wages, NCP/LOP Days and Refund of Advances. Employee PF is calculated by the tool as 12% of PF Wages, rounded to the nearest rupee. EPS and Employer EPF are then calculated by the tool. All age checkboxes start unchecked; tick only employees confirmed as above 58 years.

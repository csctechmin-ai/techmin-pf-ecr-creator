About this free online payroll software for India

Techmin Payroll Manager helps employers, payroll administrators and accountants in India run monthly salary processing in the browser. Build salary components with safe formulas, assign structures to employees, enter attendance, calculate EPF, ESI, professional tax and a gratuity estimate from dated statutory rules, and produce payslips and registers. Nothing is sent to a server.

Frequently asked questions

What is Techmin Payroll Manager?

A free, browser-based payroll workspace for Indian employers and accountants. It keeps companies, employees, salary components, attendance and monthly payroll in your own browser and produces payslips and registers.

Does my payroll data leave my computer?

No. Calculations and storage happen in your browser and nothing is sent to a server by the software. Keep regular backups, because browser data can be cleared.

Which statutory calculations does it cover?

EPF with employee, EPS, employer EPF, EDLI and admin shares, ESI with the Rs. 176 daily wage exemption, professional tax by state rule and a gratuity estimate, all from effective-dated rules you can edit.

Does it handle the EPF wage ceiling change of 17 September 2026?

Yes. When a rule changes inside a month, wages are taken for each part of the month at that part's ceiling. Confirm the rule against the latest EPFO instruction before relying on it.

Does it create the EPF ECR or ESIC upload file?

Not in this release. Use the free PF ECR File Creator and ESIC Monthly Contribution Creator on tools.complypro.co.in for those files. Upload files for the payroll manager itself are planned.

Can I use it for several companies?

Yes. Each company keeps its own employees, components, attendance, payroll runs and settings, and you switch between them from the selector at the top.

How do I back up my data?

Use Data and backup to download a JSON backup, optionally encrypted with a password, and restore it later. A backup carries an integrity check that is verified on restore.

Is it free?

Yes. A voluntary donation is welcome but creates no warranty, support or service obligation.

1. Purpose

Techmin Payroll Manager is a browser-based payroll workspace for accountants, payroll administrators and employers. It keeps companies, employees, salary components, attendance and monthly payroll in your own browser, calculates earnings, EPF, ESI and professional tax from effective-dated rules, and produces payslips and registers. Version 1.0.0 is the first release and covers the payroll core. Statutory upload files, arrears, exits, labour welfare fund, income tax, bonus and full and final settlement follow in later releases.

  • Nothing is sent to a server. Calculations, storage, Excel, CSV and PDF files are all handled in your browser.
  • Amounts are held in whole paise and rounded by explicit rules, never by loose decimal arithmetic.
  • Every statutory rate, ceiling and slab is a dated, editable rule with a source and a verified mark.

2. Who it is for

  • Payroll and accounts professionals who run salaries for one or more establishments.
  • Employers and managers of small and medium businesses and institutions.
  • Compliance consultants who prepare payroll for several clients and need clients kept apart.

4. How to use it

  1. Company: create your company. This also loads default salary components, a sample template and the statutory rules.
  2. Rule master: open each rule marked unverified, compare it with the official notification and tick Verified once confirmed. Add a professional tax rule for any state other than Kerala.
  3. Salary components and templates: adjust the components and build a template for each group of employees.
  4. Employees: add or import employees, then assign a template, gross salary and effective date. Use the selection boxes to assign many at once.
  5. Attendance and inputs: pick the month, enter loss of pay days, overtime and manual items, then save.
  6. Payroll run: calculate, read the exceptions, open each employee for the full working, mark as reviewed, approve and lock.
  7. Reports and payslips: produce registers and payslips from the saved payroll.
  8. Data and backup: download a backup after each month. Add a password if the file will be stored or sent anywhere.

5. How the main figures are worked out

  • Salary: each recurring earning is calculated for a full month and then prorated by paid days divided by the days basis. Overtime and manual items are not prorated.
  • EPF: employee 12 per cent of EPF wages; employer EPS 8.33 per cent of pension wages (nil from the age you set, 58 by default); employer EPF is 12 per cent less EPS. EDLI and admin charges are shown separately. Amounts are rounded to the nearest rupee.
  • EPF ceiling change: the wage ceiling rose from Rs. 15,000 to Rs. 25,000 on 17 September 2026. When a rule changes inside a month, wages are taken for each part of the month at that part's ceiling, weighted by days. This follows EPFO's September 2026 illustration as reported. Check the latest EPFO instruction.
  • ESI: employee 0.75 per cent and employer 3.25 per cent of ESI wages, each rounded up to the next rupee. Where average daily wages are Rs. 176 or less the employee share is nil and the employer share remains.
  • Professional tax: the state rule gives slabs on monthly, half-yearly or yearly gross and the months of deduction. Half-yearly rules sum the saved months of the period and warn if earlier months are not saved.
  • Gratuity estimate: wages for gratuity multiplied by 15, divided by 26, multiplied by counted years (a part year over six months counts as a year), subject to the limit in the rule. It is an estimate, not an accounting provision or an actuarial valuation.
  • Year to date: the sum of saved approved or locked months in the financial year (1 April to 31 March), plus any opening balance entered on the employee, plus the month being viewed.

7. Data, privacy and backup

  • Records are kept in the IndexedDB storage of your browser. Nothing is transmitted to Techmin or any other server by the software.
  • Browsers can lose this data if you clear site data, use a private window, change profile or lose the device. Download a backup regularly.
  • A backup password encrypts the file with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from the password. Techmin cannot recover a lost password.
  • Uploaded employee documents stay in the browser and are not included in the JSON backup. Keep the originals.
  • Identifiers (PAN, UAN, IP number and bank account) are masked on screen and in reports and payslips unless you tick the box to show full numbers.
  • Use trusted devices only. A browser has no server-side login, so anyone who can open this browser profile can open the data.

8. Known limitations and what comes next

  • This release does not produce the EPFO ECR file or the ESIC contribution upload file. The separate PF ECR File Creator and ESIC Monthly Contribution Creator on the same site do that from a sheet.
  • Not yet included: EPF arrears, EPF and ESI exit workflows, labour welfare fund, income tax and salary TDS (enter TDS manually for now), Form 16, statutory bonus, full and final settlement, leave policies and holiday calendars.
  • ESI contribution periods and the continued-coverage rule are not modelled. Wages above the ceiling raise a warning only.
  • The EDLI admin charge is held at zero and the minimum administrative charge per establishment is not applied. Confirm both.
  • Excel (.xlsx) reading and writing use a library loaded from the internet the first time. CSV works without it. The Excel paths were not exercised in the automated tests for this release.
  • Professional tax uses the saved months for half-yearly slabs. Where earlier months are not saved, a warning appears and the slab is applied to the months available.
  • The tool does not file returns or connect to any government portal, and its output is not an official certificate or return.

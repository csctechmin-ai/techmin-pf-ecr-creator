Free income tax calculator for Government employees in India

This calculator is made for people who draw a salary or pension from Government service: Central and State Government employees, teachers, police, railway and defence civilian staff, and public sector employees. Enter the monthly pay, DA, HRA, arrears and bonus, add your deductions, and see the old and new regime side by side for FY 2026-27 with rebate, surcharge and cess. Key rules the calculator applies

  • Standard deduction: Rs 75,000 in the new regime and Rs 50,000 in the old regime for salary and pension.
  • Rebate under section 87A: up to Rs 60,000 in the new regime for income up to Rs 12 lakh, with marginal relief; up to Rs 12,500 in the old regime for income up to Rs 5 lakh.
  • HRA exemption (old regime): the least of HRA received, 50% or 40% of basic plus DA, and rent paid less 10% of basic plus DA. Eight cities count at 50%.
  • NPS employer contribution, section 80CCD(2): allowed in both regimes, 14% of basic plus DA for Central and State Government employees.
  • Old regime deductions: 80C up to Rs 1.5 lakh (GPF, PPF, LIC, tuition fees, home loan principal), 80CCD(1B), 80D, 80DD, 80DDB, 80E, 80G, 80TTA and 80TTB, and home loan interest.
  • Arrears: relief under section 89(1) through Form 10E.

Statements and forms you can download

  • Anticipatory Income Tax Statement for the drawing and disbursing officer, with monthly TDS to recover.
  • Form 16 Part B (Annexure) computation in the standard layout.
  • Form 12BB declaration of claims for deduction of tax under section 192.
  • Form 10E working (Table A) for relief under section 89(1) on pay revision and DA arrears.
  • Office abstract of salary, tax, tax paid and balance for every employee, as CSV or PDF.

Worked example

An employee earning Rs 1,08,000 a month, with leave surrender of Rs 1,00,000, bonus of Rs 10,000 and arrears of Rs 3,00,000, has salary income of Rs 17,06,000. After the Rs 75,000 standard deduction, taxable income in the new regime is Rs 16,31,000. Tax on this is Rs 1,26,200, cess is Rs 5,048, and the total is Rs 1,31,250 after rounding to the nearest ten rupees. If Rs 60,000 has been deducted, the balance of about Rs 71,250 can be spread over the months left.

Frequently asked questions

Who can use this income tax calculator?

Any salaried or pensioner employee in India, and it is built around Government service: Central and State Government employees, teachers, police, railway and defence civilian staff, and public sector staff. It handles monthly salary, DA and pay revision arrears, bonus, leave surrender, HRA, NPS and the usual deductions.

Which financial year does it cover?

FY 2026-27 (assessment year 2027-28), which is Tax Year 2026-27 under the Income-tax Act, 2025. FY 2025-26 is also available. Both use the same slabs.

What are the new regime slabs for 2026-27?

Nil up to Rs 4 lakh, 5% from 4 to 8 lakh, 10% from 8 to 12 lakh, 15% from 12 to 16 lakh, 20% from 16 to 20 lakh, 25% from 20 to 24 lakh and 30% above 24 lakh. Salaried people get a Rs 75,000 standard deduction, and income up to Rs 12 lakh pays no tax after the rebate, with marginal relief just above it.

Does it compare the old and new regime?

Yes. Both are calculated side by side from the same entries, the lower tax is marked, and the calculator tells you how much more in deductions the old regime would need to win.

How is the HRA exemption worked out?

Over the months in which you paid rent, it is the lowest of the HRA received, 50% (Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Ahmedabad) or 40% of basic plus DA, and rent paid less 10% of basic plus DA. It applies in the old regime only.

Is NPS employer contribution deductible?

Yes, under section 80CCD(2), in both regimes. For Central and State Government employees the limit is 14% of basic plus DA. Please confirm the limit for your employer category.

Does it work out Form 10E relief for arrears?

Yes. Enter the arrears in the calculator, then open the Form 10E tab, list each earlier year with its arrears and your taxable income for that year, and it prepares Table A, the relief amount and a PDF working sheet. You can send the relief straight back to the calculator.

Can I plan monthly TDS with it?

Enter the TDS deducted in each month already processed. For the months left empty, the calculator spreads the balance tax evenly and shows the monthly amount.

Can a drawing officer handle many employees?

Yes. Save each employee in your browser, then open the abstract to get one table of salary, tax, tax paid and balance for the whole office, as CSV or PDF.

What is the Anticipatory Income Tax Statement?

It is the statement a drawing and disbursing officer collects from each employee at the start of the year, showing salary month by month, deductions, tax on total income, tax already deducted and the monthly TDS still to be recovered. The calculator prints it in the usual government layout.

Can I download Form 16 Part B and Form 12BB?

Yes. The Statement and forms tab produces Form 16 Part B (Annexure), Form 12BB and the anticipatory statement as PDFs filled from your entries. They are working documents; the signature and employer details must be completed by the person responsible for deduction of tax.

Which regime is better for a Government employee?

It depends on deductions. With a Rs 75,000 standard deduction and no tax up to Rs 12 lakh of income, the new regime is often lower for people with few deductions. The old regime can win when HRA, 80C, NPS 80CCD(1B), mediclaim and home loan interest are large. The calculator shows both and the break-even point.

Is income up to Rs 12 lakh tax-free for salaried employees?

Under the new regime for FY 2026-27, income up to Rs 12 lakh pays no tax after the section 87A rebate of up to Rs 60,000, and a salaried person with the Rs 75,000 standard deduction can have gross salary up to about Rs 12.75 lakh. Marginal relief applies just above the limit. The rebate does not apply to special-rate income.

How are DA arrears and pay revision arrears taxed?

Arrears are taxed in the year you receive them. If they relate to earlier years you can claim relief under section 89(1) by filing Form 10E. Use the Form 10E tab to work out the relief and carry it back to the calculator.

Does it work for State Government employees in Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Karnataka and other states?

Yes. Income tax rules are national, so the same slabs and deductions apply to all States. Professional tax, which differs by State, can be entered in the deductions section for the old regime.

Is my salary data safe?

The calculation runs in your browser and nothing is sent to a server. Saved employees stay in this browser, so download a backup regularly.

Is it free?

Yes. A voluntary donation is welcome but creates no warranty, support or service obligation.

How to use it

  1. Fill the employee details. Choose the age category, the employer type and whether the place of work is one of the eight HRA cities.
  2. Enter basic pay, DA, HRA and other taxable allowances month by month. Use Copy first month to all months to save time, then change the months that differ.
  3. Add arrears, bonus and other income, then the deductions you can prove. If arrears belong to earlier years, open the Form 10E relief tab to work out the relief.
  4. Enter the TDS already deducted. The result at the top updates as you type.
  5. Save the employee. Open Statement and forms to download the Anticipatory Income Tax Statement, Form 16 Part B and Form 12BB, and open Saved employees and abstract for the office-wide table.

What it calculates

  • Old and new regime side by side, with age-wise old-regime slabs.
  • Standard deduction, family pension deduction, professional tax and the HRA exemption.
  • Sections 80C group, 80CCD(1B), 80CCD(2), 80D, 80DD, 80DDB, 80U, 80E, 80EEA, 80EEB, 80G and 80TTA or 80TTB.
  • Rebate under section 87A with marginal relief, surcharge with marginal relief, 4% cess and rounding to Rs 10 under section 288B.
  • Balance tax, monthly TDS for the months left and the break-even deduction at which the old regime beats the new one.
  • Saved employees in this browser, with an office abstract as CSV or PDF, and backup and restore.

Things to know about the statements

  • Tax is rounded to the nearest ten rupees, so a total can differ by a few rupees from a worksheet that does not round. For example, Rs 1,31,248 becomes Rs 1,31,250.
  • The Anticipatory Statement has extra deduction lines (HRA, professional tax, home loan interest and Chapter VI-A) so that old regime figures add up. Form 16 Part B adds a surcharge line when surcharge applies.
  • These are working documents prepared from your entries. They are not a certificate issued by an employer. The signature, TAN and deductor details must be completed by the person responsible for deduction of tax.
  • The monthly recovery on the statement is rounded up to the next Rs 100.

What it does not do yet

  • Capital gains and other special-rate income are not modelled. Enter only income taxed at slab rates.
  • Set-off of house property loss on let-out property, the Agniveer and some rarely used deductions are not included.
  • Residents only. Non-resident rules are not applied.
  • Form 16 Part B and Form 12BB merge a few lines of the printed forms (for example SLI, GIS and post office deposits are entered under the related 80C items).

₹176 average daily wage exemption: the employee share is exempt only when the prescribed average daily wages for the wage period are ₹176 or less. This is not determined from one day's wage alone. This tool estimates average daily wages as gross wages ÷ payable days; confirm the prescribed calculation and relevant wages/days for each case before filing. The employer share remains payable.
Important: This is an independent payroll preparation tool, not an ESIC government portal or an official ESIC-approved application. Always review the generated file and follow the latest instructions on the ESIC employer portal before filing.

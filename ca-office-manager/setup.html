CA Office Manager
Firm setup guide - your data stays in your own Google account

This guide is for the partner or administrator who sets up CA Office Manager for a firm. It takes about 20 minutes and needs no programming. Staff do not follow this guide; they only open the firm link you give them.

Files for Step 2

Use Copy and paste into the Apps Script editor, or download the files.

How your data is kept

CA Office Manager is built so that each firm's data stays in that firm's own Google account.

  • You create a small private data service in your Google account (Google Apps Script). It creates a spreadsheet called CA Office Work Management - Database and a Drive folder called CA Office Management in your Drive.
  • All client records, tasks, fees, documents and email are stored there. Documents stay private in your Drive.
  • The page at tools.complypro.co.in/ca-office-manager/ is only the screen. It runs in your browser and talks directly to your data service. It has no database of its own, and Techmin does not receive, host or have access to your firm's records.
  • Techmin can see your data only if you choose to share the spreadsheet or Drive folder with Techmin.
  • Emails to your staff and clients are sent by your Gmail account.

Two honest points: the page code is published by Techmin, so updates to the page come from Techmin; and the data service runs your copy of the application code, which you can read in the Apps Script editor. If you want to control both, ask for the page files to host on your own web address.

What you need

  • A Google account that will own the firm's data. A dedicated account for the firm (for example office@yourfirm.in, or a Google Workspace account) is better than a personal one, because the firm keeps access if a person leaves.
  • A computer with Chrome or a similar browser. Allow about 20 minutes.
  • The two files from the setup page (Code_all.gs and appsscript.json): open https://tools.complypro.co.in/ca-office-manager/setup.html.

Step 1 - Create the project

  1. Sign in to the Google account that will own the data.
  2. Open https://script.google.com and choose New project.
  3. Click Untitled project at the top and rename it CA Office Manager.

Step 2 - Add the application code

  1. In the left bar choose Project Settings (the gear) and tick Show "appsscript.json" manifest file in editor.
  2. Go back to Editor. Open appsscript.json, select everything and replace it with the contents of the appsscript.json file from the setup page (use Copy there). Save.
  3. Open Code.gs, select everything and replace it with the contents of Code_all.gs from the setup page (Copy code). Save with Ctrl+S. The file is long; that is normal.

Step 3 - Deploy it as a web app

  1. Click Deploy → New deployment. Click the gear next to Select type and choose Web app.
  2. Description: CA Office Manager. Execute as: Me. Who has access: Anyone.

Anyone only lets the page reach your service; nobody can read data without signing in to the application with an activated account.

  1. Click Deploy. When Google asks to authorise, choose your account. If you see Google hasn't verified this app, choose Advanced → Go to CA Office Manager (unsafe). This is your own project, so the warning is expected. Allow access.
  2. Copy the Web app URL (it ends in /exec). Keep it: this is your workspace address.

Step 4 - Run the one-time setup

  1. In the editor, choose the function setupApplication in the function list at the top and click Run. Authorise again if asked.
  2. Setup creates the spreadsheet, the Drive folders and the first administrator account (your own Google email). It then emails your administrator activation key to your own Gmail inbox. The key is also shown in the Execution log at the bottom of the editor.
  3. If the execution log says the mail could not be sent, copy the key from the log instead.
  4. If setup stops with Set Script property ADMIN_EMAIL, open Project Settings → Script properties → Add script property, name ADMIN_EMAIL, value your own email address, save, and run setupApplication again.

Step 5 - Activate yourself

  1. Open your firm link: https://tools.complypro.co.in/ca-office-manager/?ws= followed by your workspace address (the email from setup already contains the complete link). If you open the page without the link, paste the workspace address into the box that appears and press Connect.
  2. Choose First time? Activate your account. Enter your email, the activation key and a new password (at least 10 characters with upper case, lower case and a digit). Press Activate account.
  3. Sign in. You are the Firm Administrator.

Step 6 - Set up the firm

  1. Administration → Settings: firm name, address, email, GSTIN and state, working days and hours, holidays.
  2. Administration → Settings also shows your firm link. Copy it; this is what you give your staff.
  3. Registration settings: open_registration (Y lets staff register themselves), registration_approval (Y means you approve each person before they can sign in; recommended), registration_default_role.
  4. Administration → Backup & triggers → Install triggers: switches on the daily reminders and the weekly backup.
  5. Check Administration → Permissions and adjust what each role may do.

Step 7 - Invite your staff

Give your staff the firm link. Each person:

  1. Opens the link and presses Connect.
  2. Chooses New here? Create an account, enters name and email, and registers.
  3. Receives the activation key by email from your firm's Gmail account.
  4. Chooses First time? Activate your account and sets a password.
  5. Waits for your approval if approval is on.

You approve people under Administration → Users → Approve. You can also add a person yourself (Add user) and give them the key personally.

Optional: send email from another address

By default emails come from the Google account that owns the workspace. To use another address of your firm (for example office@yourfirm.in):

  1. In Gmail for the owner account open Settings → See all settings → Accounts → Send mail as → Add another email address.
  2. Enter the address, press Next step and complete the verification Gmail asks for.
  3. In Administration → Settings set mail_from to that address and mail_from_name to the name you want shown.

If Gmail refuses the address, the application quietly sends from the account's own address and sets Reply-To to the one you chose, so nothing is lost.

Updating to a new version

  1. Download the new Code_all.gs from the setup page.
  2. In the editor replace the contents of Code.gs with it and save.
  3. Deploy → Manage deployments → pencil icon → Version: New version → Deploy. The workspace address does not change and your data is untouched.
  4. Run migrateSchema once from the editor (it adds new tabs or settings and keeps existing data).

Backups and ownership

  • Administration → Backup & triggers makes dated copies of the database in your Drive. Documents are not copied by a database backup; see the backup guide.
  • Add a second person as an owner or editor of the Apps Script project, the database spreadsheet and the Drive folder, so the firm does not depend on one login.
  • If the owner account is closed, the data is lost with it. Keep an off-account backup.

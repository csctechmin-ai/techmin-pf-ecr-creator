Questions

Do I need the internet?

Daily work runs offline on your computer. The internet is needed to set up the account, buy or renew, receive Techmin notices and for automatic backups to an online folder.

Where is my data kept? Can Techmin see it?

Your customers, bills and accounts stay on your own computer and in the backup folder you choose. Techmin receives only registration, licence and sign-in records — never your business data. See the Privacy Policy in VLEBook (Help & Legal).

Can I claim input tax credit?

Yes, if you enter your GSTIN when you buy. Techmin's tax invoice then shows your GSTIN. Check eligibility with your accountant.

What happens when my plan ends?

You get 3 grace days, then VLEBook works in free Basic mode (customers, up to 10 invoices a day, pending work and backup) until you renew. Your data is never deleted.

Can I get a refund?

See the Refund Policy in VLEBook (Help & Legal). Contact admin@techmincsc.in.

Is VLEBook a government or CSC product?

No. VLEBook is an independent product of Techmin Wealth Partners. It is not made, approved or endorsed by CSC SPV, the Kerala State IT Mission (Akshaya) or any Government department.

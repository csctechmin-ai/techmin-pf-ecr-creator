## 2. A record-keeping tool

- VLEBook **records** the services, fees, payments, AEPS and banking entries, stock and staff work of a centre. It does **not** submit applications to any portal, move money, operate AEPS or Micro-ATM devices, or connect to bank or government systems.
- Government fees, service charges, GST, TDS and commission rates are entered or configured by the centre. Rates change from time to time; the centre must check them against the latest official instructions.
- Figures, reports, profit calculations and summaries are working aids based on the entries made. They are **not** legal, tax, accounting or financial advice. Consult a qualified professional before filing returns or taking decisions.

**Books & GST.** The double-entry books, government fee clearing, GST tables, GSTR-1 JSON, tax summary and service profitability are prepared only from the entries made in VLEBook. The GSTR-1 JSON follows the GST offline-tool layout as publicly documented on the date shown in the app; GSTN publishes no official schema and may change the format — always read the GST portal's own validation report before filing. The GST payable figure and service profitability are **estimates** and do not apply ITC rules, interest or late fees. A GSTIN that passes the check-character test may still be cancelled or belong to someone else. These features are not a substitute for a Chartered Accountant or tax practitioner.

**Version 3.1.** The approximate location shown for a sign-in is derived from the IP address by a third-party service and may be wrong (it often shows the internet provider's city). TDS sections and rates shown are general guidance under the Income-tax Act, 2025; thresholds, payer type, PAN and lower-deduction certificates change them. GSTR-3B and CMP-08 figures are prepared only from VLEBook entries, do not apply ITC eligibility rules, interest or late fees, and must be checked before filing.

**Version 3.2.** The signature image printed on invoices is a picture of a handwritten signature; it is not a digital signature or an electronic signature certificate under the Information Technology Act, 2000. Invoices are prepared from the Centre's entries and settings (HSN / SAC codes, GST rates, rounding to the nearest rupee); VLEBook does not decide classification or tax and does not verify codes. An edited invoice is a revised invoice plus a credit note for the original; whether such a correction is appropriate for a given case is for the Centre and its tax adviser to decide.

**GST HSN / SAC list (version 3.2.1).** The list of 22,479 codes is taken from the GST HSN and SAC masters supplied to Techmin, with the descriptions as given there (spelling not corrected). It may be incomplete or out of date: codes, descriptions and rates change by law and notification. VLEBook only checks the format of a code and whether it appears in this list; it does not decide the correct classification, which is for the Centre and its tax adviser.

# Privacy Policy — VLEBook

Version {{version}} · Effective {{effectiveDate}}

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data **{{entity}}** ("Techmin", "we") processes when a Centre uses VLEBook, why, and the choices available. It is published in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and rules made under it, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 ("DPDP Rules").

## 1. The short version

- **Your business data never comes to Techmin.** Customers, bills, payments, applications, AEPS records, stock, staff, accounts, reports and backups are stored only on your computer and in backup files you save.
- Techmin receives only what is needed to register the Centre, run the licence, send notices and support you (section 3).
- We do not sell personal data and we do not show third-party advertising in VLEBook.

## 2. Data stored only on your computer

All records you enter in VLEBook are stored in your browser's storage (IndexedDB) on your computer and in backup files (.json / .txt) saved where you choose. Techmin has no access to them. For this data the **Centre is the data fiduciary** and is responsible for informing its customers, obtaining consent where needed, and keeping the data secure and accurate (see the Terms & Conditions, section 4).

Passwords of VLEBook users are stored only as salted hashes. The centre logo is stored with the centre settings on your computer and in your backups; it is not sent to Techmin. PDF and image tools process files on your computer; files are not uploaded.

Techmin does not store, copy or back up this data and cannot recover it. The **audit log** (who signed in, failed sign-in attempts, sign-outs, backups and changes, with dates and times) is also kept only on your computer and in your backup folder; Techmin does not receive it.

**CV & Resume Builder.** CVs made in VLEBook — including the customer's contact details, date of birth, education, work history, languages and an optional photo — are stored only on the centre's computer and in its backups. They are not sent to Techmin. The centre must collect them with the customer's knowledge, use them only to prepare the customer's CV, and print personal details such as photo, date of birth, gender or marital status only when the customer wants them.

**Application reference numbers.** Application, acknowledgement and tracking numbers that the centre records for a customer's application (for example a PAN acknowledgement number) are stored only on the centre's computer, printed on that customer's invoice and shown in the customer's ledger. They are not sent to Techmin. The centre should record them only to serve the customer and give them to no one else.

**Activity log.** From version 2.11.0 the audit log records every activity in VLEBook from sign-in onwards — screens opened, records added, changed, cancelled or deleted, prints, downloads, outside links, backups and restores — with the user name, date and time. It stays on the centre's computer and in its backup folder; staff should be told that their activity in VLEBook is recorded. The Owner can clear all transactions with **Settings → Start fresh**; this removes them from the computer only (backups already saved are not changed) and is itself written in the audit log. **More than one computer:** when the Owner turns on *Work on more than one computer*, each computer writes its changes, and a full copy of the centre's data, into the centre's own Google Drive folder, where the centre's other computers read them. Techmin receives only each computer's Device Code and a one-way code (hash) of the folder key, used to add the computer to the same centre; never the business data or the key.

If you choose a **Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox** folder for backups, the files are uploaded by that service's own app under your account and that provider's terms and privacy policy apply. Techmin has no access to that account or those files.

## 3. Data processed by Techmin

When VLEBook is online it exchanges the following with the Techmin registry:

1. **Centre registration** (when the Owner account is created): centre name and type, Owner's name, mobile number, email, full address, district, state and PIN code, and — if you enter them — the GSTIN, CSC ID and Akshaya Centre ID, as entered at registration or in Settings, and the computer's Device Code (a random code created by VLEBook; it does not contain hardware or personal identifiers).
2. **Check-in** (when VLEBook opens and every few hours): Device Code, Centre ID, VLEBook version, number of active users, current licence number, and — only if you turn on push notifications — the push address given by your browser's push service.
3. **Licence and payment records** created by Techmin: plan, validity, computers and users allowed, amount, payment mode and reference.
4. **Support communications** you send us (WhatsApp, email, phone).

Techmin may also see the IP address and technical details of each request, as logged by its hosting providers.

## 4. Why we use it (purposes and legal basis)

- To register your Centre, verify its identity (GSTIN, CSC ID, Akshaya ID), issue, renew, verify and cancel licences, raise invoices for licence fees, and prevent misuse of licence keys.
- To send service notices, renewal and payment reminders, security alerts and software update information inside VLEBook, by push notification (if allowed), WhatsApp, SMS or email.
- To provide support, including password-reset codes.
- To keep accounts and tax records and meet legal obligations.
- To understand usage at an aggregate level (for example, number of centres and versions) and improve VLEBook.

We process this data on the basis of your consent given when you accept these terms and create the Owner account, and for the legitimate uses permitted under the DPDP Act (including performance of the licence you requested and compliance with law).

## 5. Service providers

We use the following providers to run VLEBook. They process data only to provide their service:

- **Google** (Google Apps Script, Google Sheets, Gmail) — the Techmin registry and email notifications.
- **GitHub** — hosting of the VLEBook web application files.
- **Browser push services** (Google, Microsoft, Mozilla, Apple) — delivery of push notifications to your browser, if you allow them.
- **Blogger (Google)** — the Techmin blog articles shown in VLEBook are loaded directly from {{website}}.

Data may be stored on servers of these providers in or outside India, subject to applicable law.

**Links to Techmin websites and other portals.** The Service Catalogue opens outside websites, including Techmin's own sites (ComplyPro (https://www.complypro.co.in/), Finance and Accounting Tools (https://tools.complypro.co.in/), Techmin Wealth Partners (https://www.techmincsc.in/), IFSC Tracker (https://www.ifsctracker.in/)). Opening a link sends your browser's normal request to that site; VLEBook sends no centre or customer data with it. Each website has its own privacy policy.

## 6. Retention

Centre registration, licence and payment records are kept while your Centre uses VLEBook and for up to 8 years afterwards to meet accounting and tax requirements, unless a longer period is required by law. Push addresses are deleted when notifications are turned off or the push service reports them invalid.

## 7. Security

The registry is accessed over encrypted HTTPS. Licence keys and reset codes are digitally signed so they cannot be forged. Access to the Techmin console is protected by a secret key. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we act promptly on any security incident and notify you and authorities as required by law.

## 8. Your rights and choices

Under the DPDP Act you may: request a summary of personal data we process about you; ask us to correct, complete or update it; ask us to erase it where it is no longer needed (subject to legal retention); withdraw consent (this may end the licence, as registration is needed to run it); nominate another person to exercise these rights; and raise a grievance. Turn push notifications off at any time in your browser or in VLEBook. Write to the Grievance Officer below; we will respond within the period required by law. You may also approach the Data Protection Board of India.

## 9. Children

VLEBook is meant for businesses. It must not be used by persons under 18 years of age.

## 10. Changes

We may update this Policy. The current version is always available in VLEBook (Help & Legal) and at our website, with its effective date. Material changes will be notified in VLEBook. This Policy is also available in Hindi and Malayalam; if a translation differs, the English version prevails.

## 11. Grievances, data breaches and applicable law

- Complaints about personal data are acknowledged within 48 hours and resolved within one month of receipt, which is within the 90-day limit set by the DPDP Rules.
- If a personal data breach affects you, Techmin will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the DPDP Act and DPDP Rules require.
- Obligations under the DPDP Act and DPDP Rules come into force in phases. Techmin will update this Policy as they apply.
- Account setup and licence activation send the details listed in section 3 to Techmin over the internet. VLEBook does not send business records at any time.

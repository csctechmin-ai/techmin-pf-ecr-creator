# Refund & Cancellation Policy — VLEBook

Version {{version}} · Effective {{effectiveDate}}

This policy explains how payments, cancellations and refunds work for VLEBook licences sold by **{{entity}}**, {{address}}.

## 1. Free trial

Every new centre gets a **14-day free trial of the full version**. No payment is collected for the trial, so no refund arises. Please use the trial to check that VLEBook suits your centre before you pay. After the trial VLEBook continues in free Basic mode; buying a licence is never compulsory.

VLEBook is in **early access**. Please use the trial to check that it suits your centre before you pay.

## 2. Paid licences

- Licences are sold for **one month or more** (for example 1, 2, 3, 6 or 12 months), paid in advance, for the number of computers and users stated in the licence.
- The total price, including GST, and the licence period are shown before you pay. During early access, prices may change for future periods but never for a period already paid.
- A licence starts on the date shown in **Users & Licence** and is delivered to the centre's computer automatically. VLEBook shows the number of days the licence is valid for.
- There is no automatic renewal or automatic debit. When a licence ends, VLEBook changes to Basic mode until you renew.
- Any free licence that {{entity}} gives (for example during a launch offer) has no fee and so no refund.
- Techmin Wealth Partners is registered under GST (GSTIN {{gstin}}) and issues a GST tax invoice for every payment. When a refund is approved, the GST is adjusted with a credit note as GST law requires.

## 3. Refunds

1. **New purchase — 7-day refund.** If you buy a paid plan for the first time and VLEBook does not work for your centre, you may ask for a refund within **7 days of payment**. {{entity}} may first try to resolve the problem.
2. **Duplicate or excess payment.** A payment made twice, or more than the invoiced amount, is refunded in full.
3. **Failed activation.** If a paid licence cannot be activated because of a fault on {{entity}}'s side and the problem is not fixed within 7 working days of your report, the fee for that licence is refunded.
4. **Not refundable:** renewals after the licence period has started, partly used periods, upgrades already in use, and fees for licences cancelled because of misuse or breach of the Terms & Conditions.
5. Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method within **7–10 working days** of approval. Bank or gateway charges, if any, may be deducted where the law allows.

## 4. Cancellation

- You may stop using VLEBook at any time. Your licence stays valid until its end date; unused periods are not refunded except as stated above.
- Auto-renewal is not used: a plan ends on its end date unless you renew.
- {{entity}} may cancel a licence for breach of the Terms & Conditions or non-payment.
- Your data always remains on your computer. After a licence ends VLEBook works in Basic mode and you can always download a complete backup.

## 5. Services of the centre

Fees, government charges and service charges that a centre collects from its own customers are the centre's matter. {{entity}} does not collect them and cannot refund them.

This includes fees a centre charges its customers for preparing a CV or resume. The CV & Resume Builder is part of the full version; it is included in the 14-day trial and in paid licences, and no separate fee is charged for it.

Goods a centre sells to its customers (for example paper, pens or pen drives), and any return or refund of them, are also the centre's matter under its own policy. In VLEBook, cancelling the invoice returns the items to stock; it does not refund any money by itself.

Products or services bought on Techmin's other websites (ComplyPro, Finance and Accounting Tools, IFSC Tracker) follow the refund terms published on those websites. Opening them from VLEBook does not change those terms.

**Books & GST** (from version 3.0) is part of the full version; it is included in the 14-day trial and in paid licences, and no separate fee is charged for it. Refunds that a centre gives its customers (for example when an application is cancelled) are recorded in VLEBook as the centre's own entries; a government fee the portal returns to the centre is recorded as a refund from the portal. VLEBook does not move money.

Version 3.1 features (GST registration modes, notes, TDS, bank reconciliation, Tally export) are part of the same full version; no separate fee is charged for them.

Version 3.2 features (professional invoice, signature, HSN / SAC master, editing invoices, compulsory round off) are part of the same full version; no separate fee is charged for them.

## 6. How to ask for a refund

Email **{{email}}** with your centre name, Centre ID and Device Code (Users & Licence), the payment reference / UTR and the reason. You will receive an acknowledgement within 2 working days.

## 7. Grievances

If you are not satisfied, contact the Grievance Officer: {{grievanceOfficer}} · {{grievanceEmail}}. Complaints are acknowledged within 48 hours and resolved within one month of receipt. You may also contact the National Consumer Helpline (1915, consumerhelpline.gov.in).

# Refund & Cancellation Policy — VLE360

Version {{version}} · Effective {{effectiveDate}}

This policy explains how payments, cancellations and refunds work for VLE360 licences sold by **{{entity}}**, {{address}}.

## 1. Free trial

Every new centre gets a **45-day free trial of the full version**. No payment is collected for the trial, so no refund arises. Please use the trial to check that VLE360 suits your centre before you pay. After the trial VLE360 continues in free Basic mode; buying a licence is never compulsory.

VLE360 is in **early access**. Please use the trial to check that it suits your centre before you pay.

## 2. Paid licences

- Licences are sold for **one month or more** (for example 1, 2, 3, 6 or 12 months), paid in advance, for the number of computers and users stated in the licence.
- The total price, including GST, and the licence period are shown before you pay. During early access, prices may change for future periods but never for a period already paid.
- A licence starts on the date shown in **Users & Licence** and is delivered to the centre's computer automatically. VLE360 shows the number of days the licence is valid for.
- There is no automatic renewal or automatic debit. When a licence ends, VLE360 changes to Basic mode until you renew.
- Any free licence that {{entity}} gives (for example during a launch offer) has no fee and so no refund.
- Techmin Wealth Partners is registered under GST (GSTIN {{gstin}}) and issues a GST tax invoice for every payment. When a refund is approved, the GST is adjusted with a credit note as GST law requires.

- **Online payments (Razorpay).** When a refund of an online payment is approved, it is sent back through Razorpay to the same UPI ID, card or bank account; banks usually credit it within 5–7 working days. A payment that failed or was not confirmed is not charged; if money was taken for a failed payment, Razorpay normally returns it automatically within 5–7 working days. Use **Check my payment** in Users & Licence, or write to us with the order number.

## 3. Refunds

1. **New purchase — 7-day refund.** If you buy a paid plan for the first time and VLE360 does not work for your centre, you may ask for a refund within **7 days of payment**. {{entity}} may first try to resolve the problem.
2. **Duplicate or excess payment.** A payment made twice, or more than the invoiced amount, is refunded in full.
3. **Failed activation.** If a paid licence cannot be activated because of a fault on {{entity}}'s side and the problem is not fixed within 7 working days of your report, the fee for that licence is refunded.
4. **Not refundable:** renewals after the licence period has started, partly used periods, upgrades already in use, and fees for licences cancelled because of misuse or breach of the Terms & Conditions.
5. Approved refunds are paid to the original payment method within **7–10 working days** of approval. Bank or gateway charges, if any, may be deducted where the law allows.

## 4. Cancellation

- You may stop using VLE360 at any time. Your licence stays valid until its end date; unused periods are not refunded except as stated above.
- Auto-renewal is not used: a plan ends on its end date unless you renew.
- {{entity}} may cancel a licence for breach of the Terms & Conditions or non-payment.
- Your data always remains on your computer. After a licence ends VLE360 works in Basic mode and you can always download a complete backup.

## 5. Services of the centre

Fees, government charges and service charges that a centre collects from its own customers are the centre's matter. {{entity}} does not collect them and cannot refund them.

This includes fees a centre charges its customers for preparing a CV or resume. The CV & Resume Builder is part of the full version; it is included in the 45-day trial and in paid licences, and no separate fee is charged for it.

Goods a centre sells to its customers (for example paper, pens or pen drives), and any return or refund of them, are also the centre's matter under its own policy. In VLE360, cancelling the invoice returns the items to stock; it does not refund any money by itself.

Products or services bought on Techmin's other websites (ComplyPro, Finance and Accounting Tools, IFSC Tracker) follow the refund terms published on those websites. Opening them from VLE360 does not change those terms.

**Books & GST** (from version 3.0) is part of the full version; it is included in the 45-day trial and in paid licences, and no separate fee is charged for it. Refunds that a centre gives its customers (for example when an application is cancelled) are recorded in VLE360 as the centre's own entries; a government fee the portal returns to the centre is recorded as a refund from the portal. VLE360 does not move money.

Version 3.1 features (GST registration modes, notes, TDS, bank reconciliation, Tally export) are part of the same full version; no separate fee is charged for them.

Version 3.2 features (professional invoice, signature, HSN / SAC master, editing invoices, compulsory round off) are part of the same full version; no separate fee is charged for them.

The GST HSN / SAC list added in version 3.2.1 is part of the same full version; no separate fee is charged for it.

Updates, including automatic updates (version 3.2.3), are part of the licence; no separate fee is charged for them.

Opening balances and voucher editing (version 3.2.4) are part of the same full version; no separate fee is charged for them.

The PDF & Image Studio, Photo & Signature Studio and PVC ID & Print (version 3.3.0) are part of the same full version; no separate fee is charged for them. Printer hardware, cards, ribbons and paper are not supplied by Techmin.

Renewal Reminders, Original Documents & Privacy, Help & Support tickets and the Print Bridge (version 3.4.0) are included without a separate fee. Renewal Reminders are part of the full version; Original Documents & Privacy and support tickets are available in every version. WhatsApp, internet, printers and consumables are the Centre's own costs.

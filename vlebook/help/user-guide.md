# VLEBook User Guide

VLEBook is office software for CSC, Akshaya, E-Seva and Jan Seva centres: billing, customers, applications, AEPS, accounts, stock, staff commission, a CV & resume builder (including the Europass format), PDF & image tools and reports. It runs in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. **Your business data is stored only on your own computer.**

**Early access.** VLEBook is in early access: it is still being developed, features and screens may change, and some errors may remain. Keep regular backups, check important figures before you rely on them, and report problems to {{email}}.

## 1. Getting started

1. Open VLEBook at **tools.complypro.co.in/vlebook/** in **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge**, on a computer that is connected to the internet.
2. Bookmark the page (Ctrl + D) so that you can open it quickly. After the first visit, VLEBook opens and works even without internet.
3. The first screen asks you to create the **Owner account**. Fill in:
   - **Centre name**, **your name**, **mobile number** and **email** (your sign-in details are sent to this email).
   - **Type of centre**: CSC, Akshaya Centre, E-Seva, Jan Seva Kendra and so on.
   - **CSC ID** – if the centre is registered with CSC (optional).
   - **Akshaya Centre ID** – required when the type is *Akshaya Centre*.
   - **Full address** (building, street, place), **district**, **state** and the 6-digit **PIN code** – all required.
   - **GSTIN** – only if the centre has GST registration (optional). VLEBook checks that the 15 characters are valid.
   - A **user name** and **password** for signing in. Read and accept the Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, Refund Policy and Disclaimer.
4. When you click **Create**, VLEBook connects to {{entity}} to activate your centre — **this step needs the internet**. A **welcome email** then arrives with your user name, Centre ID and Device Code. The password is never sent by email.
5. VLEBook shows a **recovery code** once. Write it down or download it and keep it safe. It is the only way to reset the Owner password without help from {{entity}}.
6. Go to **Settings & Centre Setup** to check your centre details and add the invoice prefix and UPI ID. GSTIN, CSC ID and Akshaya ID can be added or corrected here later; they are printed on your invoices.
7. Upload your **centre logo** (Settings & Centre Setup → Centre logo → Upload logo). Use a PNG, JPG or WEBP image up to 3 MB. The logo appears at the top of the screen and on the dashboard, and is printed on invoices, receipts and every PDF report.

### Internet and offline use

- **Needs the internet:** creating the Owner account (activation of the centre), activating or renewing a licence, the welcome email, Techmin notices and blog articles, and push notifications.
- **Works offline:** all other work — billing, customers, applications, AEPS records, accounts, stock, reports, tools and backups. Everything is saved on this computer.
- When the computer is online again, VLEBook checks with {{entity}} for licence renewals, cancelled licences and notices.

## 2. Trial, licence and Basic mode

### 45-day full-version trial
A new centre account starts a **45-day trial of the full version**: every feature is open, for up to 3 users. The top of the screen shows *Full trial · N days left*.

### Paid licence
To continue with all features after the trial, buy a licence from {{entity}} for **1 month or more** (for example 1, 2, 3, 6 or 12 months).
- Send your **Device Code** (Users & Licence) and the payment to {{entity}}.
- The licence reaches VLEBook **automatically** the next time the computer is online. If you received a licence key instead, paste it in **Users & Licence → Activate Licence**. Activation needs the internet.
- VLEBook then shows **"Licence valid for N days"** (till the end date) at the top of the screen and in Users & Licence. A 1-month licence starting today shows about 30 days; a 2-month licence about 60 days, and so on. The number of days counts down every day.
- 7 days before the end a yellow strip reminds you to renew. After the end date there are **3 grace days** with all features (red strip).

### Basic mode — after the trial or licence ends
If the trial ends, or a licence ends and is not renewed, VLEBook does **not** lock your data. It changes to **Basic mode** for minimal daily work:

| Available in Basic mode | Needs the full version (trial or paid licence) |
|---|---|
| Dashboard (today's summary) | Services management |
| Customers – add, search, history | Accounts, expenses and day closing |
| Billing – up to **10 invoices a day**, print and share | AEPS & banking records |
| Pending work & applications | Commissions and staff commission |
| Service catalogue (view) | Stock & inventory |
| Techmin Updates, Help & Legal | Document studio, **CV & resume builder** (incl. Europass format), PDF & image tools |
| Data backup and restore (automatic backup keeps running) | Reports & profit, saved links vault |
| Settings, Users & Licence | Staff sign-in (only the **Owner** can sign in) |

Menu items that need the full version show a **FULL** tag. When you buy or renew a licence, all features and staff logins return at once; nothing is lost.

### Locked states
VLEBook is locked only if {{entity}} cancels the licence or suspends the account, or if the computer's date has been set back. Even then you can **download a full backup** of your data.

### Moving to a new computer
Take a backup, open VLEBook on the new computer (the internet is needed to set it up), restore the backup, then ask {{entity}} to move your licence to the new Device Code.

## 3. Signing in and staff accounts

- Each person signs in with their own user name and password. To sign out, click your name at the top right → **Sign out**.
- **Where you can sign in.** User names and passwords are kept on the computer (and browser) where the centre was set up, not on Techmin's server. After **Sign out** the same computer shows the **User name / Password** screen. On the Features & Plans page, click **Sign in** (top right). On a computer where VLEBook was never set up there is no sign-in box: choose **Join my centre (shared Google Drive folder)** or **Restore from backup folder**, then sign in. Do not create a new Owner account for a centre that already exists.
- The Owner adds staff in **Users & Licence → Add User** (full version). Choose a role:
  - **Owner**: everything, including users, licence, settings, reports and backup.
  - **Manager**: everything except users and licence.
  - **Operator**: counter work only — customers, billing, applications, AEPS, stock entries, studio and tools. Operators cannot cancel bills, see reports, change settings or restore backups.
- Link an operator to their **Staff Commission** record so their bills are counted under their name automatically.
- Forgot password? Staff: ask the Owner (Users & Licence → Reset password). Owner: on the sign-in screen click **Forgot password?** and enter your recovery code, or a reset code from {{entity}} support.
- Change your own password: your name at the top right → **Change my password**.

## 4. Daily work

### Customers
Add each citizen once with mobile number and name. The **360° view** shows all bills, payments, dues, advances and applications of that customer. Duplicate mobile numbers are blocked.

### Billing & Invoices
1. Type the mobile number — an existing customer is filled in; a new one is registered automatically.
2. Add one or more services. Government fee, service charge and GST are calculated from the Service Catalogue. Prices, fees and any commission in the Service Catalogue are entered by you — VLEBook does not add commission rates.
3. Choose who handled the bill (staff), the payment mode and the amount received. A part payment leaves a balance due.
4. **Generate Invoice**, then print A4 / 80 mm thermal, download PDF or share on WhatsApp. Your centre logo, GSTIN, CSC ID and Akshaya ID (when filled in) are printed on the invoice.

**The A4 invoice and its PDF** use one professional layout: centre details and logo, the document title (Tax Invoice, Bill of Supply or Invoice) with *Original for recipient* and the payment status, a *Billed to* box (with the customer's GSTIN), invoice number, date, place of supply and reverse charge (No), a table with HSN/SAC, government fee, taxable value, GST rate, CGST + SGST or IGST and amount, a GST rate summary, amount in words, totals, *For your centre — Authorised Signatory* and your thank-you note. The PDF looks the same as the printed page.

**Round off is compulsory.** Every bill is rounded to the nearest rupee: 49 paise or less goes down, 50 paise or more goes up (₹10.47 → ₹10, ₹10.50 → ₹11). The difference is shown on the bill and posted automatically to the **Round Off** ledger, which appears as a separate line in Profit & Loss (under income when the rounding gained is more, under expenses when it is less).

**Signature on invoices.** In **Settings & Centre Setup → Authorised signatory's signature**, upload a clear photo or scan of the signature (dark ink on white paper). VLEBook removes the background, crops it and fits it above *Authorised Signatory* on every A4 invoice, PDF and thermal receipt. You can add the signatory's name and designation, change or remove the signature at any time.

**Editing an invoice already issued.** In the invoice list click **Edit**. The services, customer and payment are loaded into the billing form; make the changes, type the reason and click **Save revised invoice**. VLEBook issues a revised invoice with the same date and the number *original-R1* (then R2 …) and cancels the original with a credit note — money, stock, advances and the books are reversed and posted again. Both invoices stay in the list, linked to each other, and the edit is in the audit log. Only users who may cancel invoices (Owner / Manager) can edit. A GST month marked as filed cannot be edited; issue a credit note in the current month instead.
5. A wrong bill is **cancelled**, never deleted: a credit note is made and money, commission and stock are reversed (Owner/Manager only). When a bill is cancelled, the money taken on it goes back out of **each account it came into** (cash, UPI, bank — including payments collected later), any advance used is returned to the customer, its commission and linked applications are cancelled, and Accounts, the Day Book and the Smart Counter show the same figures as before the bill. In the Day Book the entry is marked **CANCELLED** in red.

**GST bills (Tax Invoice).** From version 3.1 VLEBook chooses the kind of bill from your GST registration (see 4A): no GSTIN → non-GST bill; regular → Tax Invoice; composition → Bill of Supply. GST is charged on the **service charge only, never on the government fee**, at each service's GST rate (set in Service Management; *No GST (0%)* for services without GST). The rate of a line can be changed in the **GST %** column of that bill. The invoice then says **TAX INVOICE** and shows your GSTIN, place of supply, taxable value, CGST + SGST (or IGST), any round off and an optional SAC code. Bills already saved keep their amounts; cancel and re-issue a bill if it must change.

**Selling items from stock.** Pens, paper, files, pen drives and any other item kept in Stock & Inventory can be billed: choose the item in **Add Item from Stock** below the service picker, then type the quantity. The item's selling price is used as set in Stock & Inventory — **including GST** (GST is worked out from inside the price) or **excluding GST** (GST is added on top) — with its GST rate and HSN code. On a GST bill, GST is charged on the full value of the item. Saving the bill reduces the stock by the quantity sold; cancelling the bill puts it back. If the quantity is more than the stock, the line shows *Stock will go negative* and VLEBook asks before saving.

**Reference numbers.** Under each service line you can type the **Application No.**, **Acknowledgement No.** and **Tracking No.** given by the portal or department (for example a PAN acknowledgement number or a certificate application number). They are printed on the invoice and shown in the customer's 360° ledger. Numbers received later can be added from **Customers → 360° Ledger → Applications & reference numbers → Edit numbers** or from **Pending Work → Edit numbers**; a reprint of the invoice then shows them.

### Pending Work & Applications
Track each application from *Received* to *Delivered*, with portal reference numbers and expected dates. Overdue applications are highlighted. Send status updates on WhatsApp.

**Cancel work:** click **Cancel work** on the application and type the reason (at least 5 characters). The work is marked *Cancelled* with the reason, the user and the time; it is kept in the history and written in the audit log. A cancelled work cannot be reopened — enter it again if needed.

### AEPS & Banking
Record AEPS / Micro-ATM withdrawals, deposits, Aadhaar Pay, DMT and BBPS. Each entry shows its effect on cash in hand and on your wallet, with commission and TDS. **Never type a customer's PIN, OTP or biometric data into VLEBook.**

### Accounts & Smart Counter
Expenses, transfers and daily closing (the books in **Books & GST** follow these entries automatically). At day end count the cash with the denomination calculator; any difference must be explained before closing.

### Stock & Inventory
- **Stock Inward**: purchases, customer returns, opening stock and adjustments.
- **Stock Outward**: sales, items used in service, damaged items, returns to supplier.
- **Stock Summary** shows opening, inward, outward, closing and value for any period.
- **Stock starts empty.** Add your own items with **+ New Item** (VLEBook no longer adds sample items; unused sample items from older versions are removed automatically).
- **Edit item:** click **Edit** to change the name, prices, reorder level or supplier. To correct the quantity, type the right figure in **Correct stock to** with a **reason** — VLEBook saves it as a *Stock adjustment (+/−)* entry so the history stays complete.
- **Delete item:** click **Delete** and type the reason (Owner / Manager). Past stock entries stay in the history and the deletion is written in the audit log.
- Tick **Deduct 1 from this item for every printed page billed** on one item (for example A4 paper) to reduce it automatically when printing is billed.
- **Selling price, GST and HSN:** for each item set the selling price and choose whether it **includes** or **excludes GST**, the **GST rate that applies to that product** (you must choose it — VLEBook does not assume a rate; check with your tax adviser) and the HSN code. These are used when the item is billed; a GST bill is not saved while an item has no GST rate.
- **Physical Stock Check:** open the *Physical Stock Check* tab, choose the date (as on), and type the quantity actually counted on the shelf. If the count is **less** than the software stock (for example software 30, counted 20), the difference of 10 is recorded as **Consumption (physical check)**. If it is **more** (software 20, counted 30), the difference of 10 is added as **Difference on physical check**. Both appear in the movement ledger and the audit log.
- **Alerts:** a yellow alert reminds you when a physical stock check is due (every 30 days by default — change it on the Physical Stock Check tab). A red **Negative stock** alert appears on the dashboard and in Stock & Inventory when more was sold or used than the stock shows; record the missing purchase or do a physical check.

### Staff Commission
Set each staff member's commission (percentage or fixed per service, daily or monthly). See what is payable today or this month and record payouts.

### PDF & Image Studio (version 3.3.0)
One menu item, **PDF & Image Studio**, holds every PDF, image, photo, OCR and card tool. Everything runs on your computer — files are never uploaded and are not kept after you leave. Choose a customer and service at the top to **Log job** or **Log & add to bill**.

- **PDF Tools** — merge (Sort A–Z, drag order), split / extract, organise, compress (shows the real size saved), PDF to Word / Excel / images (JPG, PNG, WebP, chosen pages) / text, images to PDF, **add / duplicate / crop pages or change page size**, **headers, footers and stamps** (DRAFT, COPY, PAID, CANCELLED … with a date line), watermark and page numbers, **fill and flatten forms**, **properties & metadata** (view, change, remove), **password-protect (AES-256)**, unlock with the customer's password, and **Redact (secure)**. Every result can be **previewed page by page** before downloading; several results download as one ZIP. **Ctrl + Enter** runs the chosen tool.
- **PDF Editor** — *Change existing text* (edits the PDF's own text when its font allows it; otherwise VLEBook says so and nothing changes), *Text box over page* (any language), signature / image, shapes and arrows, highlight, real PDF comments, and a cover box. Zoom, search, undo / redo, and **Save As New PDF** — the original is never overwritten. A digitally signed PDF shows a warning: editing breaks its signature, and a signature picture is not a DSC.
- **Redact (secure)** — drag boxes over what must go. Each marked page is rebuilt as a picture, then VLEBook re-opens the file and checks the hidden words can no longer be found. A white cover box is *not* redaction.
- **Image Tools** — resize, compress to an exact KB limit, convert (JPG, PNG, WebP, HEIC from iPhones), crop / rotate / enhance, and GIF frames. A limit that cannot be met is reported — the file is never silently larger.
- **Photo & Signature** — see the next section.
- **OCR & Scanner** — read text from photos and scanned PDFs in **English, Malayalam and Hindi**; low-confidence words are highlighted; correct the text, then download TXT or a searchable PDF. The scanner turns phone photos of pages into one clean PDF (colour, grayscale or black & white, straighten, reorder). OCR can misread names, numbers and handwriting — always check.
- **PVC ID & Print** — design cards in millimetres (CR80 85.60 × 53.98 mm or custom), front and back, with text, photos, shapes, lines, QR codes, barcodes and masks; colour, contrast, saturation, warmth, tint, shadows, highlights and sharpness for every picture; rulers, 3 mm safe zone, bleed, snapping, lock, groups, undo / redo; save, export and import templates. **Bulk cards** from Excel / CSV / JSON using `{{Column}}` fields; photos are matched by the file name written in a column. **Customer ID print sheets** (e-PAN, e-Aadhaar, Voter ID, DL, RC, ABHA, PM-JAY, e-Shram, ration card): VLEBook suggests the card areas with a confidence score, you confirm, straighten or correct perspective, and the card is placed full size; for Aadhaar a mask over the first 8 digits is added by default. **Print** makes a 600 DPI PDF: A4 with cut marks, one card per page for card printers, or PVC-tray positions. Use the **calibration sheet** and a **printer profile** (shift, scale from the measured 100 mm lines, duplex flip edge, tray layout). Print at *Actual size / 100%*. A browser cannot see the printer, so each job shows *Print window opened* until you mark it **Done**, **Failed** or **Cancel**. A cost calculator shows material cost, charge and margin.
- **QR, Stamp & Calculators** and **Job history** — as before.

### Photo & Signature Studio
For exam, recruitment and admission portals. Tabs: **One candidate**, **Batch**, **Passport photo printing**, **Exam profiles & custom**, **Recent processing**.
1. **Select the examination** — search by name, authority, state, category or year: Kerala exams and universities, UPSC, SSC, NTA, banking, railway, defence, other central recruitment, all 28 State PSCs, Union Territories and admission categories.
2. **Each profile shows its status**: *Officially verified*, *Official portal reference*, *Previous-year reference*, *Unverified*, or **Official specification not verified** (no sizes recorded — type the sizes from the notification). Expired profiles are not used unless you confirm.
3. **Photograph** — upload, drag, paste or use the webcam; crop with shapes 1:1, 3:4, 4:5, 2:3, 3:2, 5:7 or free; zoom, rotate, straighten, face guide (and a face-finding helper where the browser offers one); brightness, contrast, colour and sharpness; even out a plain background; name and date on the photo when the exam asks. No beauty filters and no change to the face.
4. **Signature** — the signature area is found automatically; white paper, ink kept black or blue; never redrawn.
5. **Additional documents** (thumb impressions, declaration, certificates) appear only when the exam needs them.
6. **Validate** — every file gets **GREEN** (meets the recorded sizes), **AMBER** (meets them but the exam profile is not officially verified, or a manual check is needed) or **RED** (a size, format or limit is not met — the exact problem is shown). Green means the file matches the configured requirements; it does not guarantee acceptance by the authority.
7. **Download** — files are named like *Candidate_001_Kerala_PSC_Photo.jpg*; after a confirmation screen, everything downloads as one ZIP with a processing report.
File-size control: give the minimum, maximum and target KB; VLEBook keeps the exact pixel size and adjusts only the quality, shows the final bytes and compression %, warns when compression is heavy, and explains when a minimum cannot be reached (it never adds meaningless data). **Batch**: add many files named like *Asha_photo.jpg* and *Asha_sign.jpg*; the table shows Candidate | Photograph | Signature | Status (*Ready*, *Incomplete*, *Needs correction*); unmatched files are listed for you to assign. **Passport photo printing**: 35 × 45 mm, 2 × 2 inch, 25 × 35 mm, stamp size or custom, on A4 or 4 × 6 inch paper, at 300 or 600 DPI, with margins, spacing, cut marks and a print preview. **Exam profiles**: add, edit, copy a profile for a new year, disable or delete (built-in ones can only be disabled), attach the official notification PDF, record who verified it and when, compare and restore earlier versions, see the change log, export and import. Custom profiles are always marked *user-defined, not officially verified*.

### Renewal & Expiry Reminders (version 3.4.0)
Keep track of vehicle insurance, pollution certificates, vehicle tax, driving licences, permits, trade licences, certificates, subscriptions and anything else that expires, and remind the customer in time.
1. Open **Renewal Reminders** → **+ Add reminder**. Pick the customer from your customer list (or type a new name and mobile), choose the category, enter the vehicle number or document reference, the expiry date and, if different, the renewal due date.
2. Tick **The customer agreed to receive renewal reminders** only when the customer has agreed. Without consent VLEBook will not prepare a message.
3. The schedule (by default 60, 30, 15, 7, 3 and 1 days before, on the day, and 1 and 7 days after) can be changed for each reminder or for all new ones in **Categories, schedule & messages**.
4. The cards at the top show **Overdue**, **Due today**, **Next 7 days** and **Next 30 days**; the bar below shows how many reminders are due to be sent today. Filter by category, operator, customer or status; export to CSV; or use the **Calendar** tab.
5. Open a reminder → choose the message template (English, Malayalam, Hindi, or your own) → **Open WhatsApp with this message**. WhatsApp opens on your computer or phone with the text ready; **you press Send in WhatsApp**. Then click **I sent it — mark as sent**. VLEBook records *Message prepared*, *WhatsApp opened* and *Sent (confirmed by operator)* with the time and your name. It cannot see whether a message was delivered or read, and never says so.
6. When the customer renews, click **Mark renewed**: for categories that repeat (for example insurance every 12 months) a new reminder for the next period is created.
- The same item for the same customer with the same expiry is caught as a duplicate; you can open the existing one or save another on purpose.
- Changing a customer's details does not delete or change their reminders. If the mobile number changed, the reminder offers to use the new number.
- Desktop alerts (once a day) appear only after you allow them and only while VLEBook is open.
- Owners can delete reminders permanently (with a reason); renewed and cancelled reminders can be archived automatically after a set number of days.

### Original Documents & Privacy (version 3.4.0)
A register of customers' **original documents** kept by the centre, and the centre's records of **consent**, **retention** and **customer privacy requests**.
1. **+ Receive originals** — choose the customer, add each document (type, description, condition and, if needed, the ID number), the purpose, the expected return date and where it is kept. VLEBook prints a **receipt** with a barcode reference. Only the last 4 characters of an ID number are saved unless the Owner chooses otherwise in Retention Rules.
2. **Return** — open the record, enter who received it (self, family member or authorised person), how it was acknowledged (signature, OTP reference, thumb impression or verbal with a witness) and print the **return acknowledgement**.
3. **Due for Return** lists overdue originals, those due soon, those held for a long time, receipts not yet acknowledged and anything reported missing. The menu shows the number of overdue originals.
4. **Custody History** cannot be edited. Each entry is linked to the one before it, so a changed or removed entry is detected. Corrections are added by the Owner with a reason; the old entries stay.
5. **Consent Register** — one record per purpose (service, keeping originals, keeping copies, reminders, offers), the kinds of data covered, the notice shown (English, Malayalam, Hindi; editable with versions) and how it was collected. **Withdraw** applies what follows: for example renewal reminders for that customer stop.
6. **Retention Rules** — how long each kind of record is kept and what happens then (delete, remove personal details, or review). Items appear 14 days before they are due; nothing is deleted automatically. Records on **legal hold** are never deleted. Invoices and accounts are kept as tax law requires.
7. **Privacy Requests** — when a customer asks for a copy of their data, a correction, deletion or to withdraw consent: open a request, **check identity** first, then prepare the export (a ZIP with one JSON file and CSV files) or carry out the deletion (Owner only, type DELETE to confirm). VLEBook shows what will be deleted, what will be anonymised and what must be kept, and why. It cannot delete copies in old backup files, WhatsApp, email, paper files or government portals.
8. **Audit** shows the deletion log (coded references only) and the activity on these records.
These screens help the centre keep records; they are not legal advice and do not make the centre compliant with any law by themselves.

### Help & Support tickets (version 3.4.0)
Open **Help & Support** → **+ New ticket**. Choose what the problem is about and how urgent it is, write a short subject and what happened. Optional: the screen, your browser and computer type, and up to 3 screenshots. Every screenshot is shown to you first: drag on it to **black out** customer names, numbers or amounts, or crop it. Tick the privacy check and click **Send to Techmin**. VLEBook warns if your text looks like it contains an Aadhaar, PAN, mobile or bank number or a password. Drafts stay on this computer. Replies from Techmin appear in the conversation and the menu shows a badge for new replies. When Techmin marks a ticket resolved you can confirm and close it, or reopen it with a reason. Nothing else from your computer is sent.

### Printing to any printer (version 3.4.0)
Every print in VLEBook can go three ways: **Windows print dialog** (works with every printer installed in Windows — choose paper, colour, two-sided and tray there), **Download PDF**, or **Direct to a printer** with the free **VLEBook Print Bridge**.
- A web page is not allowed to see the printers installed in Windows. The Print Bridge is a small helper for the Windows computer. Install it once: **PDF & Image Studio → Print & printers → Download the Print Bridge**, unzip it and double-click **Install VLEBook Print Bridge.cmd**. It then runs in the background with an icon in the notification area (click ^ next to the clock), starts by itself with Windows, and VLEBook connects to it automatically — there is no code to type. If Chrome or Edge asks to allow access to devices on your local network, choose Allow. Right-click the icon for its status, Open VLEBook, Show log, Start with Windows, Disconnect all browsers and Quit.
- VLEBook then lists the installed printers (USB, network, virtual and card printers) and, for each, what its driver reports: paper sizes, resolutions, colour, two-sided printing, trays and the printable area. Options the driver does not report are not offered. Choose the printer, paper, orientation, copies, page range, colour, two-sided, tray and size (actual size or fit) and click **Print**.
- *Sent to Windows* means Windows accepted the job; it does not prove that the page or card came out. Use **It printed correctly** or **It did not print** to finish the job record.
- **Print & printers** also prints any PDF or picture, prints a test page with a 100 mm ruler, and lists recent print jobs.
- The Bridge works only on that computer (127.0.0.1). It connects automatically only to the VLEBook web address; any other address must pair with a 6-digit code shown in a notification. It needs no administrator rights, keeps no copy of what is printed, and can be removed with **Uninstall VLEBook Print Bridge.cmd**. VLEBook shows a notice when an older Print Bridge is running. Printing on your own printers, including PVC card printers, is to be checked by you after going live.

### PVC cards — several files, front and back, turning the card (version 3.4.0)
- **Open PDF / images** now accepts several files (and each page of a PDF). Every file stays as a thumbnail: click **Front** or **Back** under any of them, in any order, or click the thumbnail to choose a card area inside it. Nothing is replaced when you open the next file.
- **Auto-crop & resize to the PVC card** (tick box under the opened files, on by default): **Front** / **Back** finds the card in the file, crops to its edges, trims it to the card shape (85.60 × 53.98 mm for CR80) and fits it exactly to the card; a message shows how sure the match is — check the edges. Untick it to place the whole picture without cropping and resize it yourself. A placed document picture has **Resize to card**: *Auto-crop to the card edges*, *Fit exactly to the card*, *Fill the card (crop edges)*, *Whole picture (no crop)* and a **Zoom** slider (move the picture with *From left* / *From top* under Crop). When you mark an area by hand, **Keep the PVC card shape** keeps the card's width : height. A card lying the other way round is turned, never stretched.
- **Colour & quality** of the document picture: with **Auto** ticked (default) brightness, contrast, colour, sharpness and the other settings apply to the front and back document pictures together. Untick Auto to adjust each side separately, or use **Manual: copy these settings** to copy them once.
- **Turn the card** between landscape and portrait in the Card panel: **Reflow layout** keeps each item's size and moves it to the same relative place; **Rotate whole design 90°** turns everything with the card. Both sides change, nothing is deleted, and Undo brings it back.
- **Print to a printer…** sends the cards through the same print dialog: with the Print Bridge the card printer is chosen first, the CR80 card size reported by its driver is selected, front and back are sent as pages at 600 DPI with two-sided printing when the driver supports it, and the shift from your printer profile is filled in.
- **Edit profile** now opens as a clear dialog: card position (front and back shifts, two-sided flip), size correction from the calibration sheet, A4 sheet layout and PVC tray positions.

### Card designer, templates and bulk cards (version 3.4.0)
- **Tools:** text, picture, photo, QR, barcode, rectangle, **circle / ellipse**, **polygon or star**, line, **freehand drawing**, **date** (today or a set number of days ahead, in the format you choose), **serial number** (prefix, start and number of digits; each card gets the next number), **watermark** and mask. ## 4A. Books & GST (accounts)

**Books & GST** keeps full double-entry accounts for your centre. You keep working as before — billing, receipts, expenses, AEPS — and VLEBook writes every entry in the books for you. Open **Books & GST** in the menu.

### The government fee is not your income — the ₹50 example
1. A customer asks for a community certificate. You pay the **₹50 government fee** to the portal from your own current account.
2. On the bill, next to that service, choose **Govt / portal fee paid from: Current account** and type the portal transaction reference. (If you pay later, choose *Not paid to portal yet* and use **Record portal payment** afterwards.)
3. VLEBook keeps the ₹50 in **Government fee clearing**, against that customer and application. The Overview shows it under **Govt fees to recover**.
4. When the customer pays the bill (₹50 fee + your service charge), the ₹50 comes back to clearing — the balance becomes **zero** — and only your service charge is counted as income.
5. If the customer pays only part, the fee stays partly open; if the application is cancelled, any fee not recovered stays visible until it is recovered or the Owner writes it off with a reason.
Click **Fee chain** (on the bill list, in Pending Work or in the clearing list) to see the whole story for one application.

### GST registration decides the bill
- **No GSTIN** → VLEBook makes **non-GST bills** only.
- **Regular** registration → every bill is a **Tax Invoice**. VLEBook works out CGST + SGST (customer in your State) or IGST (another State) from the **place of supply** or the customer's GSTIN. You can type the customer's GSTIN on the bill (optional) so a registered customer can claim input tax credit; it is also saved in the customer record.
- **Composition** registration → every bill is a **Bill of Supply** with the line "Composition taxable person, not eligible to collect tax on supplies". No GST is collected. In **Books & GST → GST** you see the quarter's turnover and composition tax for **CMP-08**.
Set this when you register, or in **Books & GST → GST → GST profile** (GSTIN, Regular / Composition, the date registration starts, the composition rate). If your GSTIN was saved before version 3.1, VLEBook asks the Owner (or Manager / Accountant) once after the update to confirm Regular, Composition or *Not GST registered*. The same screen has the **GST rates** list: add a rate with the date it starts, edit or delete it. **HSN / SAC master** (beside GST profile) lists the HSN codes for goods (4, 6 or 8 digits) and SAC codes for services (6 digits starting with 99) your centre uses: add, edit or delete them, then choose them for each service (Service Management) and stock item (Stock & Inventory). A code in use cannot be deleted. Not shown when the centre is not GST registered. The window includes the full GST HSN / SAC list: search by code or words and click **Use** to fill the code and its official description (you may shorten the description). The list helps you find codes; choosing the correct code is the centre's responsibility (see the Disclaimer).

**Price + GST or including GST.** In Service Management choose for each service whether its price is **Price + GST** (GST added on top of the service charge) or **Price includes GST**. The form shows what the customer pays. On a bill line you can tick **incl. GST** for that line. GST is never charged on the government fee. A service can be **Exempt from GST** or **Nil rated (0%)**.

**After billing.** Use **Credit note** in the bill list to reduce the service charge on a bill that is not fully paid (wrong rate, discount). For a bill already paid, cancel it and make a new one. For suppliers use **Debit note** in Expenses.

**Returns.** In the GST tab: **GSTR-3B help** (table and JSON), **Mark GSTR-1 / GSTR-3B as filed** (new GST bills dated in a filed month are refused; the Owner can reopen with a reason), and **Record GST payment** (challan). VLEBook never files returns.

**TDS.** When you pay a supplier bill (for example rent above the TDS limit), choose the TDS section; VLEBook works out the TDS, pays the supplier the rest and shows the TDS to pay in **Reports → TDS deducted and paid**. Record the challan with **Record GST / TDS payment**.

**More tools.** **Bank reconciliation** tab (tick entries cleared in the bank statement), **Mark reviewed / Ask a question** on any voucher (for your accountant), and **Export to Tally (XML)** in Books & reports.

## 5. Backups — protect your data

**Your business data is kept only on this computer and in the backups you save. {{entity}} does not store it on any server, does not keep a copy and cannot recover it.** VLEBook gives no assurance of data safety beyond the tools described here, so please set up automatic backup on the first day.

### Automatic backup to Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox or a folder
1. Open **Data Ownership & Backup → Automatic backup**.
2. Under **Where should backups be saved?** choose **Google Drive**, **OneDrive**, **Dropbox** or **This computer / USB drive**.
3. Click **Choose backup folder** and pick a folder inside that service's folder on this computer — for example *G:\My Drive\VLEBook backups* (Google Drive for desktop), *OneDrive\VLEBook backups* or *Dropbox\VLEBook backups*. The Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox app on this computer then uploads every file to your own account.
4. Choose **Every hour** and/or fixed times (for example 1:30 pm and 8:00 pm), and JSON and/or TXT.

From then on VLEBook saves each backup to that folder **automatically, without asking**, as files named like **CentreName_14112026_083015_pm.json** and **.txt**. Every save is written in the **audit log** (for example "Hourly automatic backup saved to Google Drive folder …").

- Automatic backup runs while VLEBook is open; a backup missed while it was closed runs soon after you open it.
- After VLEBook is reopened, Chrome may ask once more for folder access: click **Reconnect backup folder** (choose *Allow on every visit* if offered). Until you do, automatic backups are paused (they are not downloaded) and the button stays at the top of the screen.
- Old automatic backups are removed after the number of days you choose (default 30). The audit-log file is never removed.
- When you close VLEBook after making changes, it offers a backup. This happens every time: save it if you want to, or ignore it.
- A yellow **Backup needed** button at the top means data changed since the last backup. Click it (or **Save Now**) to take the backup at once — to your backup folder when one is connected, otherwise to Downloads. The button then disappears, also in other open VLEBook tabs.
- **Start fresh (Settings → Start fresh, Owner only):** clears all bills, payments, day-book entries, counter closings, AEPS entries, commissions, staff payouts and stock movements, and sets account opening balances and stock quantities to zero. Users, operators, passwords, customers, services, stock items, settings, licence and the audit log are **kept**. Take the offered backup first; you must type **START FRESH** to confirm. It cannot be undone except by restoring a backup.

### Restore
- **Data Ownership & Backup → Restore**: choose a .json or .txt backup → *Validate* → *Restore*. TXT backups carry a checksum; an edited file is refused.
- **If this browser's data was cleared, or on a new computer:** on the first screen click **Restore from backup folder**, choose the same Google Drive / OneDrive / Dropbox / USB folder, and VLEBook brings back the latest backup and the full audit log. The internet is needed for this step.

### What clearing the browser does
Clearing **browsing history** or **cached images and files** does not delete VLEBook's data or its audit log. Clearing **cookies and other site data** for this site, resetting the browser or using a different Windows user does remove the copy in the browser. Your backup folder is not affected, and **Restore from backup folder** brings everything back. VLEBook also asks the browser to protect its storage (Data Ownership & Backup shows *Browser storage protection*).

At the **end of each day** enter the counter reading (photo optional). *Used vs billed* shows, for each period between readings, pages used, pages billed on the linked services (bills dated after the earlier reading up to the later one; cancelled bills excluded) and the difference, with warnings for pages not billed (more than 10 %), unusual jumps, a lower reading than before (treated as a counter roll-over — check it) and days with print bills but no reading. A **reset** or **replacement** of the counter is recorded with the old counter's last reading and a reason. **Correct** changes a reading and keeps the earlier value, who changed it, when and why. *Download as CSV* saves the report. Permissions: *Add and change printers* (Owner / Manager), *Enter print meter readings* (Owner, Manager, Operator), *Correct or reset print meter readings* (Owner / Manager). The figures are only as accurate as the readings and the linked services.

### Names and addresses in Malayalam / Hindi, and voice typing
On the customer form open **Name & address in Malayalam / Hindi (optional)**, choose the script and click **Suggest**: VLEBook offers up to four spellings of the English name or address in Malayalam or Hindi letters (transliteration — the same sounds, not a translation; common names, Kerala places and address words such as *Road*, *Near*, *Junction* come from a word list). Tap one and correct it if needed; it is saved with the customer as the local-script name / address, and the English stays the main record. This works offline. **🎤 Voice typing** (Chrome and Edge) uses the browser's speech service, which processes the audio **online**; the first time in a session VLEBook explains this and asks you to agree. What was heard is shown with alternatives for you to check; nothing is filled in until you tap *Use this*. If the microphone is refused or the browser has no voice typing, VLEBook says so and you can type. Never speak Aadhaar numbers, OTPs or passwords.

### Techmin notices for some centres or users
Techmin can send a notice only to some states or districts, app versions, roles (for example Owner and Manager) or users who can open certain screens, and can write it in Hindi and Malayalam — you see the language VLEBook is set to. Expired notices are hidden. Some notices ask you to confirm **I have read and understood**; they stay as a pop-up and unread until you do, and Techmin receives the notice number, your user name and the time of the confirmation.

## 11. Plans, online payment and invoices (version 3.4.0)
A new centre gets a **45-day free trial** (1 month + 15 days) of the full version. Then choose a plan (prices before 18% GST): **Standard** ₹249 a month or ₹2,739 a year (1 computer, 1 user), **Professional** ₹299 / ₹3,289 (2 computers, 2 users), **Premium** ₹399 / ₹4,389 (3 computers, 3 users). Yearly plans give 12 months for the price of 11. Without a plan VLEBook works in free **Basic** mode.

**To buy or renew (Owner):** Users & Licence → **Buy or renew** → choose Monthly or Yearly and a plan → check the invoice details (name, GSTIN only if you are registered, address, State, email, mobile) → the summary shows the GST (CGST + SGST in Kerala, IGST in other States) and the rounded total → **Pay with Razorpay** → pay by UPI, card, net banking or wallet. Techmin confirms the payment with Razorpay, the licence is applied at once and the **GST tax invoice with receipt** opens (Print / Save as PDF) and is emailed to you. A plan bought before the current licence ends starts the day after it ends. **My invoices** lists every invoice. If the window closed before the confirmation, use **Check my payment**. The sign-in screen and the page *All features & plans* show the plans too. Opening VLEBook on a computer where it was never set up shows the *Features & plans* page first; **Start the free trial** or **Open VLEBook** then opens the setup.

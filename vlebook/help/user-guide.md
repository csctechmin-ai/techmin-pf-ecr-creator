# VLEBook User Guide

VLEBook is office software for CSC, Akshaya, E-Seva and Jan Seva centres: billing, customers, applications, AEPS, accounts, stock, staff commission, a CV & resume builder (including the Europass format), PDF & image tools and reports. It runs in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. **Your business data is stored only on your own computer.**

**Early access.** VLEBook is in early access: it is still being developed, features and screens may change, and some errors may remain. Keep regular backups, check important figures before you rely on them, and report problems to {{email}}.

## 1. Getting started

1. Open VLEBook at **tools.complypro.co.in/vlebook/** in **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge**, on a computer that is connected to the internet.
2. Bookmark the page (Ctrl + D) so that you can open it quickly. After the first visit, VLEBook opens and works even without internet.
3. The first screen asks you to create the **Owner account**. Fill in:
   - **Centre name**, **your name**, **mobile number** and **email** (your sign-in details are sent to this email).
   - **Type of centre**: CSC, Akshaya Centre, E-Seva, Jan Seva Kendra and so on.
   - **CSC ID** – if the centre is registered with CSC (optional).
   - **Akshaya Centre ID** – required when the type is *Akshaya Centre*.
   - **Full address** (building, street, place), **district**, **state** and the 6-digit **PIN code** – all required.
   - **GSTIN** – only if the centre has GST registration (optional). VLEBook checks that the 15 characters are valid.
   - A **user name** and **password** for signing in. Read and accept the Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, Refund Policy and Disclaimer.
4. When you click **Create**, VLEBook connects to {{entity}} to activate your centre — **this step needs the internet**. A **welcome email** then arrives with your user name, Centre ID and Device Code. The password is never sent by email.
5. VLEBook shows a **recovery code** once. Write it down or download it and keep it safe. It is the only way to reset the Owner password without help from {{entity}}.
6. Go to **Settings & Centre Setup** to check your centre details and add the invoice prefix and UPI ID. GSTIN, CSC ID and Akshaya ID can be added or corrected here later; they are printed on your invoices.
7. Upload your **centre logo** (Settings & Centre Setup → Centre logo → Upload logo). Use a PNG, JPG or WEBP image up to 3 MB. The logo appears at the top of the screen and on the dashboard, and is printed on invoices, receipts and every PDF report.

### Internet and offline use

- **Needs the internet:** creating the Owner account (activation of the centre), activating or renewing a licence, the welcome email, Techmin notices and blog articles, and push notifications.
- **Works offline:** all other work — billing, customers, applications, AEPS records, accounts, stock, reports, tools and backups. Everything is saved on this computer.
- When the computer is online again, VLEBook checks with {{entity}} for licence renewals, cancelled licences and notices.

## 2. Trial, licence and Basic mode

### 14-day full-version trial
A new centre account starts a **14-day trial of the full version**: every feature is open, for up to 3 users. The top of the screen shows *Full trial · N days left*.

### Paid licence
To continue with all features after the trial, buy a licence from {{entity}} for **1 month or more** (for example 1, 2, 3, 6 or 12 months).
- Send your **Device Code** (Users & Licence) and the payment to {{entity}}.
- The licence reaches VLEBook **automatically** the next time the computer is online. If you received a licence key instead, paste it in **Users & Licence → Activate Licence**. Activation needs the internet.
- VLEBook then shows **"Licence valid for N days"** (till the end date) at the top of the screen and in Users & Licence. A 1-month licence starting today shows about 30 days; a 2-month licence about 60 days, and so on. The number of days counts down every day.
- 7 days before the end a yellow strip reminds you to renew. After the end date there are **3 grace days** with all features (red strip).

### Basic mode — after the trial or licence ends
If the trial ends, or a licence ends and is not renewed, VLEBook does **not** lock your data. It changes to **Basic mode** for minimal daily work:

| Available in Basic mode | Needs the full version (trial or paid licence) |
|---|---|
| Dashboard (today's summary) | Services management |
| Customers – add, search, history | Accounts, expenses and day closing |
| Billing – up to **10 invoices a day**, print and share | AEPS & banking records |
| Pending work & applications | Commissions and staff commission |
| Service catalogue (view) | Stock & inventory |
| Techmin Updates, Help & Legal | Document studio, **CV & resume builder** (incl. Europass format), PDF & image tools |
| Data backup and restore (automatic backup keeps running) | Reports & profit, saved links vault |
| Settings, Users & Licence | Staff sign-in (only the **Owner** can sign in) |

Menu items that need the full version show a **FULL** tag. When you buy or renew a licence, all features and staff logins return at once; nothing is lost.

### Locked states
VLEBook is locked only if {{entity}} cancels the licence or suspends the account, or if the computer's date has been set back. Even then you can **download a full backup** of your data.

### Moving to a new computer
Take a backup, open VLEBook on the new computer (the internet is needed to set it up), restore the backup, then ask {{entity}} to move your licence to the new Device Code.

## 3. Signing in and staff accounts

- Each person signs in with their own user name and password. To sign out, click your name at the top right → **Sign out**.
- The Owner adds staff in **Users & Licence → Add User** (full version). Choose a role:
  - **Owner**: everything, including users, licence, settings, reports and backup.
  - **Manager**: everything except users and licence.
  - **Operator**: counter work only — customers, billing, applications, AEPS, stock entries, studio and tools. Operators cannot cancel bills, see reports, change settings or restore backups.
- Link an operator to their **Staff Commission** record so their bills are counted under their name automatically.
- Forgot password? Staff: ask the Owner (Users & Licence → Reset password). Owner: on the sign-in screen click **Forgot password?** and enter your recovery code, or a reset code from {{entity}} support.
- Change your own password: your name at the top right → **Change my password**.

## 4. Daily work

### Customers
Add each citizen once with mobile number and name. The **360° view** shows all bills, payments, dues, advances and applications of that customer. Duplicate mobile numbers are blocked.

### Billing & Invoices
1. Type the mobile number — an existing customer is filled in; a new one is registered automatically.
2. Add one or more services. Government fee, service charge and GST are calculated from the Service Catalogue. Prices, fees and any commission in the Service Catalogue are entered by you — VLEBook does not add commission rates.
3. Choose who handled the bill (staff), the payment mode and the amount received. A part payment leaves a balance due.
4. **Generate Invoice**, then print A4 / 80 mm thermal, download PDF or share on WhatsApp. Your centre logo, GSTIN, CSC ID and Akshaya ID (when filled in) are printed on the invoice.
5. A wrong bill is **cancelled**, never deleted: a credit note is made and money, commission and stock are reversed (Owner/Manager only). When a bill is cancelled, the money taken on it goes back out of **each account it came into** (cash, UPI, bank — including payments collected later), any advance used is returned to the customer, its commission and linked applications are cancelled, and Accounts, the Day Book and the Smart Counter show the same figures as before the bill. In the Day Book the entry is marked **CANCELLED** in red.

**GST bills (Tax Invoice).** Choose *GST Intra-State* or *GST Inter-State* only if the centre is GST-registered — VLEBook asks for your GSTIN in Settings before it saves a GST bill. GST is charged on the **service charge only, never on the government fee**, at each service's GST rate (set in Service Management; *No GST (0%)* for services without GST). The rate of a line can be changed in the **GST %** column of that bill. The invoice then says **TAX INVOICE** and shows your GSTIN, place of supply, taxable value, CGST + SGST (or IGST), any round off and an optional SAC code. Bills already saved keep their amounts; cancel and re-issue a bill if it must change.

**Selling items from stock.** Pens, paper, files, pen drives and any other item kept in Stock & Inventory can be billed: choose the item in **Add Item from Stock** below the service picker, then type the quantity. The item's selling price is used as set in Stock & Inventory — **including GST** (GST is worked out from inside the price) or **excluding GST** (GST is added on top) — with its GST rate and HSN code. On a GST bill, GST is charged on the full value of the item. Saving the bill reduces the stock by the quantity sold; cancelling the bill puts it back. If the quantity is more than the stock, the line shows *Stock will go negative* and VLEBook asks before saving.

**Reference numbers.** Under each service line you can type the **Application No.**, **Acknowledgement No.** and **Tracking No.** given by the portal or department (for example a PAN acknowledgement number or a certificate application number). They are printed on the invoice and shown in the customer's 360° ledger. Numbers received later can be added from **Customers → 360° Ledger → Applications & reference numbers → Edit numbers** or from **Pending Work → Edit numbers**; a reprint of the invoice then shows them.

### Pending Work & Applications
Track each application from *Received* to *Delivered*, with portal reference numbers and expected dates. Overdue applications are highlighted. Send status updates on WhatsApp.

**Cancel work:** click **Cancel work** on the application and type the reason (at least 5 characters). The work is marked *Cancelled* with the reason, the user and the time; it is kept in the history and written in the audit log. A cancelled work cannot be reopened — enter it again if needed.

### AEPS & Banking
Record AEPS / Micro-ATM withdrawals, deposits, Aadhaar Pay, DMT and BBPS. Each entry shows its effect on cash in hand and on your wallet, with commission and TDS. **Never type a customer's PIN, OTP or biometric data into VLEBook.**

### Accounts & Smart Counter
Expenses, transfers and daily closing. At day end count the cash with the denomination calculator; any difference must be explained before closing.

### Stock & Inventory
- **Stock Inward**: purchases, customer returns, opening stock and adjustments.
- **Stock Outward**: sales, items used in service, damaged items, returns to supplier.
- **Stock Summary** shows opening, inward, outward, closing and value for any period.
- **Stock starts empty.** Add your own items with **+ New Item** (VLEBook no longer adds sample items; unused sample items from older versions are removed automatically).
- **Edit item:** click **Edit** to change the name, prices, reorder level or supplier. To correct the quantity, type the right figure in **Correct stock to** with a **reason** — VLEBook saves it as a *Stock adjustment (+/−)* entry so the history stays complete.
- **Delete item:** click **Delete** and type the reason (Owner / Manager). Past stock entries stay in the history and the deletion is written in the audit log.
- Tick **Deduct 1 from this item for every printed page billed** on one item (for example A4 paper) to reduce it automatically when printing is billed.
- **Selling price, GST and HSN:** for each item set the selling price and choose whether it **includes** or **excludes GST**, the **GST rate that applies to that product** (you must choose it — VLEBook does not assume a rate; check with your tax adviser) and the HSN code. These are used when the item is billed; a GST bill is not saved while an item has no GST rate.
- **Physical Stock Check:** open the *Physical Stock Check* tab, choose the date (as on), and type the quantity actually counted on the shelf. If the count is **less** than the software stock (for example software 30, counted 20), the difference of 10 is recorded as **Consumption (physical check)**. If it is **more** (software 20, counted 30), the difference of 10 is added as **Difference on physical check**. Both appear in the movement ledger and the audit log.
- **Alerts:** a yellow alert reminds you when a physical stock check is due (every 30 days by default — change it on the Physical Stock Check tab). A red **Negative stock** alert appears on the dashboard and in Stock & Inventory when more was sold or used than the stock shows; record the missing purchase or do a physical check.

### Staff Commission
Set each staff member's commission (percentage or fixed per service, daily or monthly). See what is payable today or this month and record payouts.

### PDF & Image Tools and Studio
Merge, split, compress, edit and convert PDFs; resize photos and signatures to portal sizes; make passport photo sheets and QR codes. Files are processed on your computer and never uploaded.

### CV & Resume Builder (full version)
Make professional CVs for customers at the counter, using **only the details the customer gives**.
1. Open **CV & Resume Builder** → **+ New resume** or **+ New Europass CV**.
2. Answer the questions step by step: *Personal details* (you can fill from an existing customer), *Job goal & profile*, *Work experience*, *Education & training*, *Skills*, *Languages*, *Certificates, projects & more* and *Declaration & finish*. The live preview on the right updates as you type.
3. **Profile summary:** type your own, or click **Build summary from my answers** — it is made only from the answers given and can be edited. Nothing is invented.
4. Click **Show designs to customer**: the customer sees **their own CV** in every design — Classic, Modern sidebar, Professional, Executive, Minimal, Job-portal (ATS) friendly and **Europass format** — and in six colours. Select the design the customer likes, type the customer's name and click **Customer approved this design**. If the CV is changed after approval, it goes back to *Draft* and must be shown again.
5. **Print / Save as PDF** (choose *Save as PDF* in the print window) or download a **Word copy** for further editing.
- The **Europass format** follows the European CV layout: personal information, about me, work experience, education and training (with optional EQF level), mother tongue and the **CEFR language grid** (A1–C2 for listening, reading, spoken production, spoken interaction and writing), digital skills, driving licence and additional information. VLEBook is not connected with the European Union or the official Europass service; customers applying through europass.europa.eu can use the official editor.
- Photo, date of birth, gender and marital status are printed only if you tick them. Many employers abroad prefer CVs without them.
- CVs are saved on this computer with the rest of your data and included in backups. Creating, approving, printing, Word copies and deleting a CV (with a reason) are written in the audit log.

### Reports & Profit BI
Daily, monthly and yearly reports of income, true service profit, commissions, dues, AEPS and stock. Export to CSV, Excel or PDF.

### Government Service Catalogue & Portal Directory
Keep the official portal links your centre uses, with the documents needed, processing time and your fee guide. Use the tabs to filter by group or state, and **+ Add** your own links (only full web addresses starting with https:// or http:// are accepted). Your own links can be changed with **Edit** (the form on the right fills in; click **Save Changes**, or **Cancel edit**) and removed with **Delete** after you confirm. Every addition, change and deletion is written in the audit log.

The **Techmin** tab lists Techmin's websites: ComplyPro (https://www.complypro.co.in/), Finance and Accounting Tools (https://tools.complypro.co.in/), Techmin Wealth Partners (https://www.techmincsc.in/), IFSC Tracker (https://www.ifsctracker.in/). These four links are built into VLEBook, are always available and cannot be edited or deleted. They are also in the **Techmin** menu on the left (available in every mode).

## 5. Backups — protect your data

**Your business data is kept only on this computer and in the backups you save. {{entity}} does not store it on any server, does not keep a copy and cannot recover it.** VLEBook gives no assurance of data safety beyond the tools described here, so please set up automatic backup on the first day.

### Automatic backup to Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox or a folder
1. Open **Data Ownership & Backup → Automatic backup**.
2. Under **Where should backups be saved?** choose **Google Drive**, **OneDrive**, **Dropbox** or **This computer / USB drive**.
3. Click **Choose backup folder** and pick a folder inside that service's folder on this computer — for example *G:\My Drive\VLEBook backups* (Google Drive for desktop), *OneDrive\VLEBook backups* or *Dropbox\VLEBook backups*. The Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox app on this computer then uploads every file to your own account.
4. Choose **Every hour** and/or fixed times (for example 1:30 pm and 8:00 pm), and JSON and/or TXT.

From then on VLEBook saves each backup to that folder **automatically, without asking**, as files named like **CentreName_14112026_083015_pm.json** and **.txt**. Every save is written in the **audit log** (for example "Hourly automatic backup saved to Google Drive folder …").

- Automatic backup runs while VLEBook is open; a backup missed while it was closed runs soon after you open it.
- After VLEBook is reopened, Chrome may ask once more for folder access: click **Reconnect backup folder** (choose *Allow on every visit* if offered). Until you do, automatic backups are paused (they are not downloaded) and the button stays at the top of the screen.
- Old automatic backups are removed after the number of days you choose (default 30). The audit-log file is never removed.
- When you close VLEBook after making changes, it offers a backup. This happens every time: save it if you want to, or ignore it.
- A yellow **Backup needed** button at the top means data changed since the last backup. Click it (or **Save Now**) to take the backup at once — to your backup folder when one is connected, otherwise to Downloads. The button then disappears, also in other open VLEBook tabs.
- **Start fresh (Settings → Start fresh, Owner only):** clears all bills, payments, day-book entries, counter closings, AEPS entries, commissions, staff payouts and stock movements, and sets account opening balances and stock quantities to zero. Users, operators, passwords, customers, services, stock items, settings, licence and the audit log are **kept**. Take the offered backup first; you must type **START FRESH** to confirm. It cannot be undone except by restoring a backup.

### Restore
- **Data Ownership & Backup → Restore**: choose a .json or .txt backup → *Validate* → *Restore*. TXT backups carry a checksum; an edited file is refused.
- **If this browser's data was cleared, or on a new computer:** on the first screen click **Restore from backup folder**, choose the same Google Drive / OneDrive / Dropbox / USB folder, and VLEBook brings back the latest backup and the full audit log. The internet is needed for this step.

### What clearing the browser does
Clearing **browsing history** or **cached images and files** does not delete VLEBook's data or its audit log. Clearing **cookies and other site data** for this site, resetting the browser or using a different Windows user does remove the copy in the browser. Your backup folder is not affected, and **Restore from backup folder** brings everything back. VLEBook also asks the browser to protect its storage (Data Ownership & Backup shows *Browser storage protection*).

## 5B. Working on more than one computer

Staff can work at the same time on two or more computers. Every computer keeps the full data and works offline; changes are shared through the centre's **Google Drive backup folder**. Your data goes only to your own Google Drive, never to Techmin.
1. On the main computer (Owner): install **Google Drive for desktop**, connect a Google Drive folder in **Data Ownership & Backup → Automatic backup**, then click **Turn on** under **Work on more than one computer** (internet needed). This computer becomes counter **A**.
2. On each other computer: install Google Drive for desktop with the **same Google account**, open VLEBook in Chrome or Edge, click **Join my centre (shared Google Drive folder)** on the first screen and choose the same folder. Wait a minute after step 1 so Google Drive can upload. Staff then sign in with their own user name and password. The computer becomes counter B, C and so on.
3. Ask {{entity}} to issue the licence for **Any computer**, so every computer gets the full version; otherwise a joined computer runs as a trial or in Basic mode (Owner only).
- Changes reach the other computers in about a minute. The **Sync** button at the top shows the counter letter and the last sync time; *Sync paused* means the folder needs **Reconnect backup folder** (Chrome asks once after VLEBook is reopened).
- Each computer has its own invoice series, for example **VLE-A-1001** and **VLE-B-1001**. Stock sold on different computers adds up; a record changed on two computers keeps the later change (both are in the audit log); different settings changed on two computers are both kept.
- **Start fresh** on one computer clears transactions on every computer. **Restoring a backup** or **Reset Local Database** changes only the computer where it is done.
- Users and passwords are shared: a staff login added on one computer works on all of them.

## 5A. Audit log

VLEBook records **every activity from sign-in onwards** with the user name, date and time: every sign-in (and every failed attempt) and sign-out, every screen opened, every record added, changed, cancelled or deleted (bills, payments, customers, services, applications, stock, CVs, settings and so on), every print, file download and outside link opened, and every backup, restore and data reset. See it in **Settings → Audit log** — search by user, activity, screen or date — and download the full log with **Download full audit log (CSV)**.

- Entries cannot be edited or deleted in VLEBook. A data reset or a restore keeps them.
- Each entry is linked to the one before it by a code (hash). **Chain check: intact** means no entry was changed or removed outside VLEBook.
- When a backup folder is connected, the audit log is also kept there as *CentreName_audit_log.jsonl*. This file only grows, and it brings every entry back after the browser's data is cleared.

## 6. Techmin Updates and notifications

- The bell at the top shows notices from {{entity}} (software updates, offers, reminders) and articles from our blog. Click **↻ Check for new updates** in Techmin Updates at any time: VLEBook says *VLEBook is up to date (version …)* or *New version … is available* with an **Update now** button (your data stays on this computer; only the program files are reloaded). If the check cannot reach the server, it says so.
- Important notices pop up when VLEBook opens.
- Turn on **push notifications** in Techmin Updates to receive notices on your computer even when VLEBook is closed (Chrome or Edge must be running).
- **After every update** VLEBook shows each user, once, what is new in that version (Help & Legal → Release Notes has the full list).

## 8. Good practices

- Use a separate Windows user account and a strong password for the computer.
- Do not use VLEBook in private/incognito windows — data there is erased when the window closes.
- Do not clear "Cookies and site data" for the VLEBook address.
- Keep Chrome/Edge up to date.
- Sign out when you leave the counter, or set an auto-lock PIN in Settings.

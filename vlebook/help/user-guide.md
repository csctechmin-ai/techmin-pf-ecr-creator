# VLEBook User Guide

VLEBook is the complete office software for CSC, Akshaya, E-Seva and Jan Seva centres: billing, customers, applications, AEPS, accounts, stock, staff commission, PDF & image tools and reports. It runs in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. **Your business data is stored only on your own computer.**

## 1. Getting started

1. Open VLEBook at the address given by {{entity}} in **Google Chrome** or **Microsoft Edge**.
2. Install it as an app: click the install icon at the right end of the address bar (or menu ⋮ → *Install VLEBook*). It then opens from the desktop like any program and works offline.
3. The first screen asks you to create the **Owner account**: centre name, your name, mobile, **email**, a user name and a password. Read and accept the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.
4. When the computer is online, your centre is registered with {{entity}} and a **welcome email** arrives with your user name, Centre ID, Device Code and licence key. The password is never sent by email.
5. VLEBook shows a **recovery code** once. Write it down or download it and keep it safe. It is the only way to reset the Owner password without help from {{entity}}.
6. Go to **Settings & Centre Setup** and fill in your centre details, GSTIN (if any), invoice prefix and UPI ID.
7. Upload your **centre logo** (Settings & Centre Setup → Centre logo → Upload logo). Use a PNG, JPG or WEBP image up to 3 MB; a square or wide logo on a white or transparent background looks best. The logo appears at the top of the screen and on the dashboard, and is printed on invoices, receipts and every PDF report. Use *Change logo* or *Remove logo* at any time.

> A new installation runs as a **14-day free trial**. To continue after that, buy a licence from {{entity}} (see section 3).

## 2. Signing in and staff accounts

- Each person signs in with their own user name and password. Signing out: click your name at the top right → **Sign out**.
- The Owner adds staff in **Users & Licence → Add User**. Choose a role:
  - **Owner**: everything, including users, licence, settings, reports and backup.
  - **Manager**: everything except users and licence.
  - **Operator**: counter work only — customers, billing, applications, AEPS, stock entries, studio and tools. Operators cannot cancel bills, see reports, change settings or restore backups.
- Link an operator to their **Staff Commission** record so their bills are counted under their name automatically.
- Forgot password? Staff: ask the Owner (Users & Licence → Reset password). Owner: on the sign-in screen click **Forgot password?** and enter your recovery code, or a reset code from {{entity}} support.
- Change your own password: your name at the top right → **Change my password**.

## 3. Licence and renewal

- Open **Users & Licence** to see your plan, valid-till date, users allowed and this computer's **Device Code**.
- To buy or renew, send your Device Code and payment to {{entity}}. When your licence is issued it reaches VLEBook **automatically** the next time the computer is online. If you were sent a licence key instead, paste it in **Users & Licence → Activate Licence**.
- 7 days before expiry a yellow strip appears at the top. After expiry there are 3 days of grace (red strip); then VLEBook locks until it is renewed. **Even when locked you can download a full backup of your data.**
- Moving to a new computer? First take a backup, install VLEBook on the new computer, restore the backup, then ask {{entity}} to move your licence to the new Device Code.

## 4. Daily work

### Customers
Add each citizen once with mobile number and name. The **360° view** shows all bills, payments, dues, advances and applications of that customer. Duplicate mobile numbers are blocked.

### Billing & Invoices
1. Type the mobile number — an existing customer is filled in; a new one is registered automatically.
2. Add one or more services. Government fee, service charge and GST are calculated from the Service Catalogue.
3. Choose who handled the bill (staff), the payment mode and the amount received. A part payment leaves a balance due.
4. **Generate Invoice**, then print A4 / 80 mm thermal, download PDF or share on WhatsApp. Your centre logo is printed at the top (in black and white on thermal receipts).
5. A wrong bill is **cancelled**, never deleted: a credit note is made and money, commission and stock are reversed (Owner/Manager only).

### Pending Work & Applications
Track each application from *Received* to *Delivered*, with portal reference numbers and expected dates. Overdue applications are highlighted. Send status updates on WhatsApp.

### AEPS & Banking
Record AEPS / Micro-ATM withdrawals, deposits, Aadhaar Pay, DMT and BBPS. Each entry shows its effect on cash in hand and on your wallet, with commission and TDS. **Never type a customer's PIN, OTP or biometric data into VLEBook.**

### Accounts & Smart Counter
Expenses, transfers and daily closing. At day end count the cash with the denomination calculator; any difference must be explained before closing.

### Stock & Inventory
- **Stock Inward**: purchases, customer returns, opening stock and adjustments. Choose "Paid from" to record the purchase in accounts.
- **Stock Outward**: sales, items used in service, damaged items, returns to supplier.
- **Stock Summary** shows opening, inward, outward, closing and value for any period. A4 paper used in billing is deducted automatically.

### Staff Commission
Set each staff member's commission (percentage or fixed per service, daily or monthly). See what is payable today or this month and record payouts.

### PDF & Image Tools and Studio
Merge, split, compress, edit and convert PDFs; resize photos and signatures to portal sizes; make passport photo sheets and QR codes. Files are processed on your computer and never uploaded.

### Reports & Profit BI
Daily, monthly and yearly reports of income, true service profit, commissions, dues, AEPS and stock. Export to CSV, Excel or PDF.

## 5. Backups — protect your data

Your data lives in this browser on this computer. If the computer fails or browser data is cleared, only a backup can bring it back.

- **Automatic backup** (Data Ownership & Backup → Automatic backup): every hour and/or at times you choose (for example 1:30 pm and 8:00 pm). Each backup is saved as two files named like **CentreName_14112026_083015_pm.json** and **.txt**.
- Click **Choose backup folder** once (Chrome/Edge) — for example a folder in *Documents*, a USB/external drive, or a folder synced by Google Drive / OneDrive. Without a folder, backups go to your *Downloads* folder.
- After VLEBook is reopened, Chrome may ask again for folder access: click **📁 Reconnect backup folder** at the top.
- Automatic backup runs while VLEBook is open. A backup missed while it was closed runs shortly after you open it.
- Old automatic backups in the folder are removed after the number of days you choose (default 30).
- **Restore**: Data Ownership & Backup → choose a .json or .txt backup → *Validate* → *Restore*. TXT backups are protected with a checksum; an edited file is refused.
- Keep at least one recent backup **outside** this computer (pen drive, external disk or cloud folder).

## 6. Techmin Updates and notifications

- The 🔔 bell at the top shows notices from {{entity}} (software updates, offers, reminders) and articles from our blog.
- Important notices pop up when VLEBook opens.
- Turn on **push notifications** in Techmin Updates to receive notices on your computer even when VLEBook is closed (Chrome or Edge must be running).

## 7. Languages, appearance and logo

Switch between English, हिन्दी and മലയാളം from the language box at the top. Your own entries (names, service names) stay as typed. Settings → Appearance offers Zoho-Books-style and ERPNext-style colours and Night mode. Your own centre logo is set in Settings & Centre Setup (see section 1).

## 8. Good practices

- Use a separate Windows user account and a strong password for the computer.
- Do not use VLEBook in private/incognito windows — data there is erased when the window closes.
- Do not clear "Cookies and site data" for the VLEBook address.
- Keep Chrome/Edge up to date.
- Sign out when you leave the counter, or set an auto-lock PIN in Settings.
